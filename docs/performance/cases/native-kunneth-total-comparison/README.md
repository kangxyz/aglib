# The original total Kunneth comparison

The original signed external product and the original total linear Kunneth
operator compiled as the same map on the main-based source recorded here.
The separate homogeneous-value producer also compiled. This case records the
complete variable-family comparison, its inferred native proof interface,
visibility repairs, and the measured frontend limits. It does not close the
native group coalgebra, coherent H1 bound, or unconditional principal consumer.

## Mathematical correspondence

For the original field and schemes, retain the original structure-sheaf
cohomology and structural scalar action. Write

\[
 A_X=\bigoplus_n H^n(X,\mathcal O_X),\qquad
 E=E_{\pi},\qquad L=(\bigoplus_n\kappa_n)E.
\]

Here the fibers are the original `HomologicalComplex₂.TotalDegree`, and `pi`
is the original `ComplexShape.π` on the three upward natural-number shapes.
The existing signed source equivalence is `s`, and the existing signed algebra
map is `Phi`. For qcqs factors, the chosen `C` uses exactly this `E` and the
one inverse family of the original degreewise `kappa`. No second inverse of
the total map or native algebra equivalence is constructed.

The two closed producer equations are:

- The original graded external-product law identifies `Phi.linear o s.linear`
  after each pair of homogeneous inclusions with the original external tensor
  map and the original output inclusion.
- The already typed total operator satisfies
  `DirectSum.MapFactorization L E kappa`. Its degreewise component after the
  original fiber inclusion is that same external tensor map.

The [lower generic comparison](../../../../Aglib/Algebra/Graded/TensorProduct/Comparison.lean)
does the tensor/direct-sum generation, composition, evaluation and transitivity
over variable module families. It consumes the actual `E` with its defining
equation and returns the literal supplied `L`. The
[native comparison](../../../../Aglib/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/StructureSheaf/KunnethComparison.lean)
then infers its maps from those two original producers. The compiled forward
equation for the same chosen `C` gives the qcqs corollary. Finally,
[Values](../../../../Aglib/AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/StructureSheaf/KunnethLinear/Values.lean)
evaluates the whole equations to recover the original homogeneous external cup.

The signed equality is valid without the qcqs assumptions; those assumptions
enter the chosen inverse and its corollary. The group/H1 consumers remain a
separate proof obligation in this evidence snapshot.

## Native interface and ownership

Keep standalone inference of the actual proof families before applying the
generic comparison. The retained native proof aliases use term `let` bindings
for the original signed family and original factorization. The result is the
actual original-map equality, not an opaque substitute proposition.

Two concrete equality headers and differently ordered applications failed
before the retained native producer passed. Their locations alone did not
identify a unique reduction pair. A bounded diagnostic found a closed original
graded producer while the expected helper input still contained metavariables.
Its raw parent-structure differences were not a verdict that the actions or
cohomology models differed.

The exposed inferred proof alias initially could not resolve the private
generic helper. The public generic theorem was moved, with its proof, into its
scheme-free lower owner; its private auxiliary proof stayed there. The native
original-kappa component theorem was made public. The protected total-tensor,
typed Kunneth and original graded producers were not edited for this repair.

The in-package consumer uses the actual inferred whole equations at arbitrary
original tensors and retains the explicit original homogeneous-value example.
Its first source failure was a missing `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme` opening for
the original `structureCohomology` name. That was repaired separately from the
production proofs.
