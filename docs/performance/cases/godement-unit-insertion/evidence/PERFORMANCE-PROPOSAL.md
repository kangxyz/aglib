# Godement open-base-change performance case proposal

The final native family passes at the ordinary 200000-heartbeat budget, with
unchanged pins, trust settings and native resource profile. Its nine native
leaves, generic factory and compatibility facade have matching coverage in
`CPP-COVERAGE.json`; exact source snapshots and the 29-name/11-map-definition
correspondence are in the adjacent source indexes.

## Validated method

Keep complete general constructions and required value laws at the weakest
sufficient layer. For each native claim, supply only the actual maps and
conditions its proof uses. In this case the successful first-square factory
constructs the full comparison/augmentation square and evaluates it through a
genuine functor before native specialization. The original geometric unit law
is its independently proved input; the desired final span is not assumed.

Retain faithful native views where they already express the proved law cheaply
enough. The final second-square proofs are the original augmentation/bridge
views. The component pastes the passed original squares in the actual module
category. The whole Plus equality then uses the original faithful inclusion
and equality in every degree; the two final consequences retain their original
proofs and hypotheses. The failed large full-Plus adapters were not adopted.

Separate stable constructors from independent expensive proofs in actual
compilation units. Basic/Geometry can proceed independently; the first square
needs no Bridge/SpanData or derived-model choice. After those producers pass,
repairing a second-square/component/factorized proof reuses their artifacts.
The final owning-root run compiled only Span and the import-only facade.

## Negative evidence and limits

The earlier passing monolith48cabc required1898.434s for its full frontend
(1913.249s including native-check preparation). The attempted large whole-Plus
native candidate dde71 failed after2723.483s of frontend time, with heartbeat
and Kernel memory diagnostics; it was not a performance success. Imported Span
probes separately identified inference/final conversion and substantial Kernel
cost. Their scheduling/instrumentation/context differences prevent treating
those timings as the original full-module baseline.

The split first graph f0e024e2 retained six fresh passes while SecondSquare and
Span failed. Later ordinary runs repaired only those uncovered proof leaves.
The source versions, invalid-control boundaries, failed conversions and
withdrawn unexecuted probes remain indexed in `CASE-INPUTS.json` and the author
results. Raw expression differences do not prove different mathematical models
or a unique compiler cost cause.

The demonstrated outcome is source-correct modular compilation and incremental
reuse. No matched cold-build speedup, full-Aglib pass, standalone lint/audit
result or hosted-CI outcome is claimed here. Some individual leaves still take
several minutes. Do not add the separate row timings and present them as a
measured cold-build duration.

## Scope and publication

All29 original public headers and11 original public definition bodies are
preserved. The two original named standard-derived-category constants occur
once in Basic; necessary leaves activate those existing constants. Independent
worlds, actual maps, original open-immersion/cartesian hypotheses and final hK
remain unchanged. No budget increase, model replacement or new mathematical
premise was used.

Root owns maintained PERFORMANCE prose/case integration and P publication.
The native entry artifact adds nine leaf imports; the generic leaf also needs
its existing enrollment when transferring to a newer root. Apply additive
imports while preserving foreign work. Remaining entry/text/DECLLINT and broader
publication coverage are distinct from the completed native family CPP.
