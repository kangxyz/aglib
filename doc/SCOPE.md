# Mathematical scope

The library contains general commutative algebra, module-sheaf theory and arbitrary-dimensional scheme geometry needed by its current consumers. A statement's complete hypotheses, objects and dependencies determine its owner, including when it was extracted from a curve or K-theory source.

The ring layer contains actual algebra descent data/equalizers, finite-field models, nilpotent purely inseparable overlaps, integral closedness, regular-local parameters/domain/normality, prime heights, tensor-domain results, support, prime filtrations, localized length and scalar Euler expressions. Its generic scalar homology, fraction and filtration prerequisites sit in their own lower subjects; the multiplicity layer consumes them.

The categorical and algebraic layers contain selected exact structures, exact functors, abelian full subcategories, small models, finite modules, scalar restriction/localization, supplied injective resolutions, Ext-map comparison and first-quadrant total complexes. Topological sheaves and generic site Cech interfaces lie below scheme-specialized cohomology.

The geometric module layer distinguishes ambient structure-module sheaves, quasicoherent sheaves, finite type, finite presentation, finite local freeness, coherent sheaves under local Noetherianity, invertible sheaves and differential sheaves. It preserves actual restriction, pullback, pushforward, stalk, tensor, support and base-change maps. A property of a sheaf and an abelian/exact structure on its full category have separate hypotheses.

Scheme geometry includes schemes over fields, actual stalks, normal components, geometric connectedness, group objects, finite-surjective geometric-point lifting, etale neighborhoods and smooth regularity/dimension in arbitrary relative dimension. Ordinary Picard groups are isomorphism classes of actual rank-one module sheaves. Differential sheaves are sheafified Kahler differentials with their universal derivations.

Scheme cohomology uses actual Zariski `Sheaf.H`, with structural scalar actions on field schemes, canonical functoriality, injective/flasque and affine comparisons, Cech comparison, finite-support cohomology, long exact sequences, field base change, proper finiteness inputs and Noetherian dimension vanishing. General topology/site results remain in their lower owners; generic long exactness is distinct from low-degree Euler/dimension consequences.

Curve and projective-line applications, degree and point counts, genus, divisors/curve degree maps, Riemann–Roch, curve-specific Euler/product formulae, K-theory, G-theory, Q/S constructions, spectra and perfect-complex applications are deferred. Generic prerequisites extracted from those sources introduce no production import of the application layer. The ordinary Picard group supplies neither Picard-scheme representability nor a curve-degree map.

Field-linear results keep their field-scheme structure. The arbitrary-base relative evaluation and module-base-change maps do not generalize every field-linear theorem to arbitrary base rings. Explicit finiteness, local-basis/rank and relative-base-change families retain the conditions in [INTERFACES.md](INTERFACES.md); they are part of the conditional interface rather than new unconditional results.

The authoritative latest local Quillen additions include generic site/scheme tensor, coefficient and cartesian-open base change, finite-projective affine sections, actual projection formulas, bounded-below Godement/derived direct-image interfaces and the selected generic D+ triangle/truncation/contraction closure. Canonical finite-type pushforward belongs below the guarded locally Noetherian coherent category.

Four precise general cuts are admitted from application owners: qcqs affine induction, the actual polynomial zero section, good-truncation factorization in an arbitrary abelian category with original scheme specialization, and cartesian opens-range equality. Selected lower prerequisites also supply the generic identity-cone functor, exact-functor derived homology, locally split tensor exactness, ordinary module-complex restriction/tensor/acyclicity, actual Godement carrier/unit and open-span comparisons, and D+ open-cover reflection. Their original containing application modules are not imported wholesale. Remaining perfect/projective-line application surfaces stay outside this scope. Godement categorical total exactness is valid on ordinary complexes; augmentation/QI/computational derived statements retain their own bounds and flasque/unit conditions.
