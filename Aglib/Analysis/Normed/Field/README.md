# Normed field consequences of cyclic unit quotients

`CyclicQuotient.lean` extends the
[pure unit-quotient normal form](../../../FieldTheory/Units/README.md).
An actual unit with norm less than one has injective integer powers; the
constructed period is also small. With algebraic closedness and characteristic
zero, the actual power-map kernel gives the finite subgroup's cardinality.
Smallness of a descended original-field period follows from its own power
relation, without a norm-comparison premise between the fields.

No completed algebraic closure, curve, or analytic uniformization is constructed.
