# Principal scalar Euler expressions

`Module.principalEuler t` in [Basic](Basic.lean) is the integer difference of the `ENat.toNat` casts of the scalar cokernel and kernel lengths. It is defined for an arbitrary module over a commutative ring, with no finite-length premise. `principalEuler_zero_of_finite_length` gives cancellation for finite total length. `principalEuler_of_regular` removes the kernel when scalar multiplication is regular; finite quotient length remains a separate condition.

`Module.FinitePrincipal` is defined in [Basic](Basic.lean) as the two actual finite scalar-length conditions. [Additivity](Additivity.lean) uses the lower actual `Module.ScalarKernel`, `ScalarCokernel` and their induced maps. Euler additivity retains finite scalar homology and the actual exact-sequence hypotheses. Generic scalar maps and six-term exactness belong to [ScalarHomology](../../ScalarHomology/README.md); finite-length cancellation belongs to [Length](../../Length/README.md).

[Filtration](Filtration.lean) evaluates the expression on actual filtration factors, preserving their finite-homology premises. [Support](Support.lean) derives finite scalar homology from actual support under Noetherian/finite-module assumptions. [Associativity](Associativity.lean) gives `Module.principalCokernel_associativity`: for a finite module over a Noetherian local ring, a scalar in the maximal ideal which is regular on that module and has finite-length cokernel gives a quotient-length formula. The sum over actual minimal support primes weights the `A`-length of the scalar quotient of `A/p` by the localized length of `M` at `p`. These local, Noetherian, finite-module, regular-scalar and finite-cokernel conditions remain explicit. [Interchange](Interchange.lean) retains the scalar-regular and finite-homology conditions for the double-cokernel comparison.

[Localization](Localization.lean) gives `Module.principalEuler_localization_of_surjective` on the actual transported scalar homology, using [surjective localization length](../../Localization/README.md). Focused imports follow the seven `Aglib.RingTheory.Multiplicity.PrincipalEuler` leaves. The internal consumers are the finite-support/filtration/associativity chain; no arithmetic-character, Grayson cycle or full principal-face theorem is claimed.
