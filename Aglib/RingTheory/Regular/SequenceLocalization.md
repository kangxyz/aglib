# Regular sequences in the original localizations

`SequenceLocalization.lean` owns the generic colon-clearing and denominator
mechanisms formerly private in `Excellent/J2Criterion`. There is one proof
implementation; the excellence consumer uses thin specializations.

## Interfaces and hypotheses

- `IsLocalization.isSMulRegular_quotient_of_mul_mem` evaluates the actual
  bound `a*x ∈ I → g*x ∈ I`, with `g` in the given localization monoid. It
  proves regularity of the original image of `a` on the quotient by the
  original mapped ideal. The source and localization ring universes are
  independent. No finiteness, Noetherianity, prime, localness, domain, field,
  or extra flatness hypothesis is required.
- `IsLocalization.exists_notMem_mul_mem_of_isSMulRegular_quotient_atPrime`
  derives such a bound outside the original prime from localized quotient
  regularity. Only the actual colon `I.comap (mulLeft a)` must be finitely
  generated. Its denominator is supplied by the existing
  `Ideal.exists_mul_mem_of_map_le_atPrime`, not a duplicate construction.
- `RingTheory.Sequence.exists_integer_weaklyRegular_list` clears one common
  denominator of the supplied localized list. It retains the list length,
  weak regularity, and exact generated ideal. The original algebra and
  localization map are fixed; the two ring universes are independent.
- `RingTheory.Sequence.exists_notMem_mul_mem_prefix_of_atPrime` produces
  one common bound for all original prefix colons, assuming their finite
  generation. `isWeaklyRegular_of_mul_mem_prefix` evaluates those same
  bounds in any specified localization inverting the bound.
- `RingTheory.Sequence.exists_weaklyRegular_atPrime_of_weaklyRegular_atPrime`
  recovers the former nearby-prime interface under Noetherianity by the same
  construction. It is not another proof of spreading.

The unit-scaling and quotient-congruence helpers remain private. Their original
proofs moved once from the excellence owner. The fraction proof retains native
`mk'` and `mk'_mem_map_algebraMap_iff`; there is no all-stalk detection theorem,
iterated Away model, or replacement algebra structure.
