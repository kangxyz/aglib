point interfaces consumed by `Marked/Successor`. It imports the unchanged
prefix and does not require access to its private declarations.

homogeneity argument uses the homogeneous linear submodule directly. The

including the lift decomposition and the original localization map. Each
actual saturated chart specializes that whole producer once.
