# Actual Case-II marked successors and local entry

This child imports `Marked/Chart`. It assembles the corrected local
continuation and entry alternatives using the original scalar and residue
maps of literal saturated-chart prime localizations.

`marked_continuation` derives the repeated rational point from the same fixed
cubic and actual child kernel, recenters the actual fraction and kernel,
and produces a corrected marked cubic with the same original exceptional
element. It also retains the original unit coefficient, minimality, extended
center ideal, and bijection of the original residue map. On the opposite
chart, `marked_opposite_parameter_not_mem` excludes the missing point through
its actual repeated-prime criterion and the original simple-root coefficient.

`triple_entry` derives the actual first point and its exact aligned triple
identity. `triple_entry_next` uses the fixed first-child cubic and genuine
normality of that child's ordinary blowup. Its normalized cone is constructed
from `TripleEntryData.tangent` through `T.presentation`, rather than supplied
again. A nonzero beta marks the first child. For zero beta, the actual second
origin and its full canonical kernel are derived before the exact second
chart equation produces the new marker `r=t/u`. The twice-mapped original
unit coefficient and original residue map remain explicit. The preceding
extended center ideal is generated by `u`; the persistent marked tail starts
with `r` at this second child.

`entry_reduction` gives a marker at the original stage or the first triple
identity. Combined with `triple_entry_next`, the local alternatives occur at
stages 0, 1, or 2. The old parameter chart of a triple stage is excluded by
`no_continuing_parameter_chart`, using the same fixed nonzero linear cubic.
No global path structure is introduced here.

The first and second localized entry constructions are carried out over a
variable source ring and its native prime localization. Each complete
producer transports the original equation, uses the actual prime's three
displayed generators, applies the full canonical-kernel hypothesis, and
returns the marker or aligned triple with minimality. The concrete saturated
chart supplies that equation and prime identity once. Its original scalar
and residue maps are retained when the local conclusions are assembled.
Recentered and replaced coefficients are linked by explicit projection
identities, including the coefficient transported through the residue map.

The fixed cubic's nonvanishing must come from the normal-chart producer
with those same coefficients. Normal ordinary blowups, the genuine full
Case-II canonical kernels, and the stated dimension, singularity, and domain
conditions retain their geometric suppliers. No conclusion-shaped kernel,
normality supplier, square-divisibility premise, or desired marked transition
is introduced. The square-alignment coefficient is recorded in the actual
child ring; no equality with a specified parent-ring lift is claimed.

The actual scheme adapter still has to identify the selected stalks with
these literal localizations, prove the original-arrow equation, and realize
the two-chart cover. It can then assemble the already defined path Marking;
the arc and termination package is reused.

These local successors retain the corrected cubic conditions stated above.
The stronger cubic forms excluded by those conditions are not asserted.
No RDP or global resolution theorem follows from this local module alone.
