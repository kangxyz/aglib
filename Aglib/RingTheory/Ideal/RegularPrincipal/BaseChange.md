# Base change of regular principal kernels

`BaseChange.lean` proves that the existing `ModuleCat.extendedKernelMap` is an
isomorphism on a commutative square of arbitrary commutative rings when the
source kernel is generated by a regular element `r` and the target kernel is
generated by the regular image of `r`. The target-kernel equality remains an
explicit premise: for a cartesian square of closed immersions, its geometric
consumer proves this equality using the actual inverse-image ideal formula.

The proof compares the canonical map with multiplication by the two displayed
generators and the original scalar-extension unit map. It uses neither
flatness nor a Noetherian assumption and does not introduce a parallel
base-change map. Its current consumers are the Cartier ideal operations.
