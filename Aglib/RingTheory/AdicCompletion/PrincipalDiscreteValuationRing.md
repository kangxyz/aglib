# Completion at a regular principal maximal ideal

Let B be a commutative local ring, and let p be an element satisfying

```text
maximalIdeal B = Ideal.span {p},
p ∈ nonZeroDivisors B.
```

The carrier is the literal `C = AdicCompletion (Ideal.span {p}) B` with
the original `algebraMap B C`. The module proves that C is a complete DVR,
that the image of p is a uniformizer, and that the original completion map
induces an isomorphism of residue fields. B is not assumed Noetherian,
separated, or a domain. Flatness of the completion map and perfectness of
the residue field are not premises.

`isSMulRegular_algebraMap_span_singleton` is the main new algebraic step.
It applies to any commutative B and any B-regular p, without locality.
Suppose `algebraMap B C p * u = 0`. For every n choose a representative b
of the actual `(n+1)`-st quotient coordinate of u. Evaluation gives

```text
p*b ∈ (p)^(n+1).
```

Write `p*b = p^(n+1)*c` and cancel the original regular element p in B.
Then `b = p^n*c`. The actual quotient transition therefore sends the
`(n+1)`-st coordinate of u to zero in order n. Compatibility shows that
every coordinate of u is zero, hence u is zero. This proof does not
substitute a flat-completion theorem for coordinate cancellation.

Finite generation of `(p)` supplies the local structure of C through the
native completion APIs. `maximalIdeal_span_singleton` identifies its actual
maximal ideal with the span of `algebraMap B C p`, and
`isAdicComplete_maximalIdeal_span_singleton` establishes completeness for
that maximal ideal. `algebraMap_isLocalHom_span_singleton` proves that the
original coefficient map is local.

Regularity of the image of p implies that none of its powers is zero.
The existing [SeparatedPrincipal](../LocalRing/SeparatedPrincipal.lean)
theorems then provide unit–power factorizations, domain structure, and the
DVR structure from the actual principal maximal ideal and Hausdorff
separatedness. The resulting public conclusions are
`isDomain_span_singleton`, `isDiscreteValuationRing_span_singleton`, and
`irreducible_algebraMap_span_singleton`. The DVR statement binds the proved
domain structure explicitly; it does not ask the caller to assume it.

`residueFieldMap_span_singleton_bijective` concerns the literal map

```text
ResidueField.map (algebraMap B C).
```

Its surjectivity uses the canonical first projection of C and its native
FG-ideal kernel formula. `residueFieldEquivSpanSingleton` is the inverse
of that actual map, and `residueFieldEquivSpanSingleton_map` records the
inverse equation on every original residue class. The residue comparison
only needs the principal maximal-ideal hypothesis; regularity of p is
needed for the domain and DVR conclusions. Injectivity of B → C is not
asserted for a possibly nonseparated B.

The current consumer is the Cohen-ring construction in R.F2. Starting with
an actual flat local residue enlargement of the p-adic integers, whose
maximal ideal is generated by p, this leaf supplies the complete DVR step.
It does not construct that residue enlargement, a coefficient embedding
into an arbitrary complete local ring, or the final finite regular subring.

This is an additive native-inventory correction to the fixed R.F2 research
packet: the separated principal unit-factorization/domain/DVR supplier was

work is coordinate regularity in the literal completion and the FG/local/
