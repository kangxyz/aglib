# Normal-domain completion from the actual regular completion map

`AdicCompletion.normalDomain_of_regularMap_of_dim_eq_two` assumes a normal
Noetherian local domain R of dimension two and regularity of its original
map to `AdicCompletion (maximalIdeal R) R`. Both domainhood and integral
closedness are proved for this identical completion.

The regular-map consequences provide regularity at all completion primes
contracting to zero, and reducedness of every actual prime-base quotient.
The generalized existing normal-surface argument supplies regular punctured
spectrum, using normality of the height-one source localizations and the
DVR associated-prime criterion. The original regular parameter pair survives
flat completion and proves integral closedness. Reducedness comes from the
injection into the regular generic fibre. Finally the genuine total quotient
ring is Artinian, and the local integral-closedness/idempotent argument gives
domainhood.

No zero-dimensionality is imposed on generic formal fibres; no complete
source, finite residue-field presentation, or Nagata hypothesis is required.
This is not yet normal completion of every normalization stalk of a
quasi-excellent surface: transferring the actual regular completion map to
a chosen local branch of the finite normalization remains a separate theorem.
Nor does this statement prove resolution existence.
