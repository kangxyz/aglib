# Principal residue kernels at directed limits

Let I be a nonempty directed preorder, let Gᵢ be commutative local rings,
and let fᵢⱼ be ring transition maps. Suppose there are compatible maps
`gᵢ : Gᵢ →+* K` into an actual field K. The carrier is the native

```text
D = Ring.DirectLimit G (fun i j h => f i j h),
ρ = Ring.DirectLimit.lift G f K g compatibility.
```

`isLocalRing_of_fieldPoint` proves D local. The actual map ρ to K supplies
nontriviality. Every element of D has a representative in one component;
one of that representative and its complement to one is a unit in the
local component, hence its image is a unit in D.

If the gᵢ are jointly surjective, `fieldPoint_surjective` proves ρ
surjective. Fix a natural p, and assume each actual kernel is the ideal
generated by `(p : Gᵢ)`. `ker_fieldPoint_eq_span_natCast` then proves

```text
ker ρ = Ideal.span {(p : D)}.
```

An element of this kernel has a representative x with gᵢ(x)=0. The
component kernel equation writes x=p y, and the actual component map gives
the required divisibility witness in D. Conversely ρ(p)=0, as already holds
in any component. `maximalIdeal_eq_span_natCast` uses the actual surjective
field point to identify this ideal with the maximal ideal of D.

For a coherent directed system, `isSMulRegular_natCast` proves regularity
of p in D whenever p is regular in every component. If p u=0, represent
u by x in Gᵢ. Native `Ring.DirectLimit.of.zero_exact` gives a later j where
fᵢⱼ(p x)=0. Ring maps preserve p, so regularity in Gⱼ gives fᵢⱼ(x)=0.
The actual direct-limit relation then gives u=0. No injectivity or
flatness of the transition maps is assumed.

`residueEquivOfFieldPoint` identifies the actual residue field of D with
K. Its equation `residueEquivOfFieldPoint_residue_of` states that each
original component element has precisely its specified gᵢ-residue. Thus
the comparison is fixed by the compatible points, rather than chosen as
an unrelated field isomorphism.

The current consumer is the limit step in an arbitrary-residue Cohen-ring
construction. The passed
[`PrincipalDiscreteValuationRing`](../AdicCompletion/PrincipalDiscreteValuationRing.lean)
theorem can be applied to this literal D, its proved principal maximal
ideal, and its proved regular parameter to obtain the complete DVR limit.
D itself is not assumed Noetherian, and flatness of its completion map is
not used.

The limit operation is not the entire Cohen construction. An actual
coherent transfinite succession of marked residue extensions must still be
constructed, as must the compatible Cohen-quotient maps into a given
complete mixed-characteristic local ring. This leaf does not assume that
succession or conclude the full R.F2 finite regular-subring theorem.
