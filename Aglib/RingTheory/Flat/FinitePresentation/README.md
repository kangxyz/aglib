# Finite-presentation prerequisites for flat principal quotients

These leaves supply the finite relation and local-model proof infrastructure for the public theorem `Algebra.regular_and_flat_of_fibre_regular` in [PrincipalQuotient/Fibrewise](../PrincipalQuotient/README.md). Their native owner is `Algebra.FlatApproximation`. They are proved technical prerequisites with the original individual hypotheses, expressed through the native `Module.Flat` and `IsSMulRegular` properties.

| Focused import | Actual construction or result |
| --- | --- |
| `Aglib.RingTheory.Flat.FinitePresentation.Relations` | Finite tensor obstructions and finitely many compatible linear lifts descend to a finitely generated intermediate coefficient algebra. Finite residue syzygies suffice for all old residue relations; the resulting map is the inclusion of the actual extended ideal. |
| `Aglib.RingTheory.Flat.FinitePresentation.Localization` | Localized module maps commute with the actual tensor maps. Canonical tensor/quotient/localization equivalences preserve mapped ideals, denominators and scalar towers, including the further localization to an actual prime local ring. |
| `Aglib.RingTheory.Flat.FinitePresentation.LocalModel` | `exists_chosen_flat_local_model` constructs a local Noetherian polynomial presentation carrying a prescribed element of an arbitrary flat finitely presented algebra over a local base. A single finite coefficient stage has the required local quotient flatness at every point over the chosen base point. It does not assert that the whole finite stage is globally flat. |
| `Aglib.RingTheory.Flat.FinitePresentation.LocalRegularity` | Regularity of the limiting residue equation descends through the actual residue-field extension to the selected Noetherian local model. Regularity and flatness of its principal quotient transport to the actual prime local ring of the original algebra. |
| `Aglib.RingTheory.Flat.FinitePresentation.FiniteStage` | The stage map, reconstructed quotient presentation and denominators agree. `regular_and_flat_at_prime_of_residue_regular` gives regularity and quotient flatness at a prime over the closed point of an arbitrary local base for a flat finitely presented algebra. |

`PrimeQuotientFlat` and `PrimeQuotientRegularFlat` are once-defined technical abbreviating propositions about `Module.Flat` and `IsSMulRegular` on the displayed canonical localizations and quotients. Their suppliers are proved in this closure. They add no class or alternative implementation of those native properties. The local Noetherian criteria, source-finiteness conditions and scalar-tower instances stay on the exact intermediate results that use them; the final arbitrary-base supplier does not acquire them.

The dependency direction is lower [tensor/adic/residue module criteria](../LocalCriterion/README.md), finite relations and localization, actual local model, local regularity, finite stage, then the public principal-quotient supplier. All imports in this closure are Mathlib or these precise lower Aglib leaves; there are no scheme or application imports.
