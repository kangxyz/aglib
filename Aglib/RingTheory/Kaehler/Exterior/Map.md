# Exterior Kähler maps and localization

For a compatible tower of commutative algebras, `mapExterior` and `mapExteriorBaseChange` are the original exterior map and its scalar extension. Generator and tensor formulas, whole composition and identity laws, and the localization property use these same maps. Localization retains its stated submonoid and `IsLocalization` hypothesis; independent scalar and universe data remain explicit.

The same degree-one tower identity `exterior_map_tower_apply` is shared by the exterior laws and the trace consumers.
