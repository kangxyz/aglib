# Effective descent of actual algebras

Import `Aglib.RingTheory.Descent.Basic` for `Algebra.Descent.Datum`: a multiplicative coaction with actual counit and coassociativity. `Datum.invariants` is its equalizer subalgebra, and `Datum.baseChange` is the actual reconstruction map.

`Datum.baseChangeEquiv` requires flatness of the scalar extension. [Canonical](Canonical.lean) gives recovery of an arbitrary original algebra from its canonical datum under faithful flatness. [Morphisms](Morphisms.lean) constructs actual invariant algebra maps, proves reconstruction naturality and transports finite/etale properties under their stated faithful-flat hypotheses. [Overlap](Overlap.lean) converts normalized tensor-overlap isomorphisms satisfying the cocycle equation into coaction data.

[Nilpotent](Nilpotent.lean) constructs formally etale descent data by actual nilpotent lifting. [PurelyInseparable](PurelyInseparable/README.md) applies this to finite purely inseparable field extensions and arbitrary affine base algebras; [FieldOfDefinition](FieldOfDefinition/README.md) builds finite-stage algebra models.

The lower overlap/equalizer results live in [TensorProduct](../TensorProduct/README.md). The internal consumers are canonical recovery, invariant morphisms and finite-field property transport. Source module paths, rather than original application names, identify the focused imports.

[FinitePresentation](FinitePresentation.lean) descends properties of modules rather than algebras: `Module.FinitePresentation.of_finitePresentation_tensorProduct_of_faithfullyFlat` proves that `M` is finitely presented over `A` when `B ⊗[A] M` is finitely presented over a faithfully flat `A`-algebra `B` (Stacks 03C4 (2), Lemma 10.83.2, stated there for `M ⊗_R S`; here the base change is written `B ⊗[A] M`). Mathlib supplies the companion descents used with it: finite generation (`Module.Finite.of_finite_tensorProduct_of_faithfullyFlat`), flatness (`Module.Flat.of_flat_tensorProduct`), bijectivity of linear maps (`Module.FaithfullyFlat.lTensor_bijective_iff_bijective`, with `LinearMap.baseChange_eq_ltensor`) and reflection of isomorphisms by `ModuleCat.extendScalars` (`ModuleCat.reflectsIsomorphisms_extendScalars_of_faithfullyFlat`). [Invertible](Invertible.lean) proves `Module.Invertible.of_invertible_tensorProduct_of_faithfullyFlat` and `Module.Invertible.iff_invertible_tensorProduct_of_faithfullyFlat`: `M` is invertible over `A` if and only if `B ⊗[A] M` is invertible over `B`. Compare Stacks 05B2 (fpqc descent of finite local freeness); the proof here does not use it, but descends finite presentation and the bijectivity of the contraction: the descended module is finitely presented, its dual commutes with the flat base change (`Module.FinitePresentation.isBaseChange_map`) and the contraction descends by faithful flatness. The synonym `Module.Invertible.DualAddCommGroup` only re-exposes the additive-group structure of the dual for Mathlib's faithfully flat criterion. Effective descent of modules is in [Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Descent](../../Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/Descent/README.md).

[Ideal](Ideal.lean) descends ideals. Over a flat `A`-algebra `B`, `Ideal.map_comap_algebraMap_eq_comap_includeLeft_map_includeRight` identifies the extension of `J.comap (algebraMap A B)` with the preimage under `includeLeft` of the extension of `J` along `includeRight`. Over a faithfully flat `B`, an ideal `J` with `J.map includeLeft = J.map includeRight` in `B ⊗[A] B` is the extension of `J.comap (algebraMap A B)` (`Ideal.map_comap_algebraMap_eq_self_of_faithfullyFlat`), and that ideal is the unique one with this extension (`Ideal.existsUnique_map_algebraMap_eq_of_faithfullyFlat`; uniqueness is Mathlib's `Ideal.comap_map_eq_self_of_faithfullyFlat`). `Ideal.exists_map_algebraMap_eq_iff_map_includeLeft_eq_map_includeRight` adds the converse. `Ideal.map_comap_eq_self_of_isPushout` states the same descent for any pushout square of a faithfully flat map along itself in `CommRingCat`; this is the form consumed by [closed-subscheme descent](../../AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/IdealSheaf/README.md). The statement is the affine case of Nitsure, Lemma 6.7(2) (arXiv:math/0504590v1). Compare Stacks Project Tag 023N, effective descent of modules along faithfully flat ring maps, which the proof does not use.
