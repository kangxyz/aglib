# Algebra models over finite field stages

[Presentation](Presentation.lean), imported as `Aglib.RingTheory.Descent.FieldOfDefinition.Presentation`, uses `Algebra.Descent.FieldOfDefinition.stageField`, `stageRing` and `FPModel`. A stage field is generated by an actual finite subset of the extension field. For a finitely presented algebra over the scalar-extended base, `finitePresentationModel` gives a model and its actual reconstruction equivalence.

[Etale](Etale.lean) transports finite-module and etale properties to the model through the actual tensor coefficient map. When the field extension is algebraic, the finite-stage field is a finite extension and `finiteEtaleModel` supplies the finite-etale model interface. Algebraicity is retained for that conclusion; no Noetherian, separability or nontrivial-base-algebra assumption is inserted.

The internal consumers are model property transport and finite-etale field-stage construction. Lower prerequisites are tensor products, finite presentation and faithful-flat descent. The focused imports are the two leaves in this subfamily.
