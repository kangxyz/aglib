# Fitting ideals

Fitting ideals of finitely generated modules over arbitrary commutative rings, covering parts of
Stacks Project, Section 15.8 (Tags 07Z6–07ZD); the uncovered items are listed under
[Not covered](#not-covered). No Noetherian or finite-presentation hypothesis is used unless a
statement names one.

## Prerequisites and consumers

- Lower prerequisites: Mathlib (minors, adjugates, `Module.Finite.exists_fin'`, right exactness
  of the tensor product, `Module.freeLocus` and `Module.rankAtStalk`) and the Aglib leaf
  [`Aglib.LinearAlgebra.Multilinear.Span`](../../LinearAlgebra/Multilinear/Span.lean)
  (`MultilinearMap.map_mem_span_of_forall_mem_span`).
- Present consumers: the tests `AglibTest.RingTheory.FittingIdeal.Basic` and
  `AglibTest.RingTheory.FittingIdeal.LocallyFree` only.
- Planned consumer: the flattening stratification of plan row F12 (Nitsure, *Construction of
  Hilbert and Quot schemes*, arXiv:math/0504590v1, Theorem 4.3, pp. 19–20), which is not yet
  formalized.

## Objects

- `Matrix.minorIdeal A r` for `A : Matrix m n R`: the ideal `I_r(A)` generated by the `r × r`
  minors `(A.submatrix e c).det`, over all functions `e : Fin r → m`, `c : Fin r → n`
  (Tag 07Z7). Non-injective choices give vanishing minors, so `I_0(A) = R` and `I_r(A) = 0`
  once `r` exceeds the number of rows or columns.
- `Submodule.minorIdeal N r` for `N : Submodule R (ι → R)`: the ideal generated by the
  `r × r` minors of all matrices with columns in `N`, written
  `(Matrix.of fun j i => v j (e i)).det` with `v j ∈ N`.
- `Module.fittingIdeal R M k : Ideal R`: for `Module.Finite R M`, the minor ideal of size
  `n - k` of the kernel of the presentation `(Fin n → R) →ₗ[R] M` chosen by
  `Module.Finite.exists_fin'` (Tags 07Z8, 07Z9); `⊥` when `M` is not finitely generated
  (`Module.fittingIdeal_of_not_finite`). Every result below that needs finite generation carries
  `[Module.Finite R M]` or a finite generating family.

## Main results

Import `Aglib.RingTheory.FittingIdeal.Stratification` for everything.

[Minors](Minors.lean):

- `Submodule.span_minorIdeal`, `Matrix.minorIdeal_eq_minorIdeal_span`: minors of a generating
  set generate, so `I_r(A)` depends only on the column span of `A` (multilinearity step of 07Z8,
  through `MultilinearMap.map_mem_span_of_forall_mem_span`).
- `Submodule.minorIdeal_succ_le`, `Submodule.minorIdeal_antitone` (07Z7 (1)),
  `Submodule.minorIdeal_mono`, `Submodule.minorIdeal_zero`, `Submodule.minorIdeal_bot_succ`,
  `Submodule.minorIdeal_one_eq_bot_iff`, `Submodule.det_smul_mem_of_surjective` (adjugate).
- `Submodule.minorIdeal_map_funLeft_le`, `Submodule.minorIdeal_map_funLeft_equiv`,
  `Submodule.minorIdeal_ker_comp_funLeft`: renaming coordinates.
- `Submodule.minorIdeal_ker_succ`: for `h : (Option ι → R) →ₗ[R] M` with `h ∘ₗ E` surjective,
  `(ker h).minorIdeal (r + 1) = (ker (h ∘ₗ E)).minorIdeal r` (adding one generator, the key
  step of 07Z8), where `E = (LinearEquiv.piOptionEquivProd R).symm.toLinearMap ∘ₗ
  LinearMap.inr R R (ι → R) : (ι → R) →ₗ[R] Option ι → R` is extension by zero.

[Basic](Basic.lean):

- `Submodule.minorIdeal_ker_eq_of_surjective`: for surjections `f : (ι → R) →ₗ[R] M`,
  `g : (κ → R) →ₗ[R] M` with `ι`, `κ` finite,
  `(ker f).minorIdeal (Nat.card ι - k) = (ker g).minorIdeal (Nat.card κ - k)` (07Z8).
- `Module.fittingIdeal_eq_minorIdeal_ker` (`[Finite ι]`, `f` surjective):
  `fittingIdeal R M k = (ker f).minorIdeal (Nat.card ι - k)`; and
  `Module.fittingIdeal_eq_minorIdeal`: `= A.minorIdeal (Nat.card ι - k)` for any relation
  matrix `A : Matrix ι J R` with `span R (Set.range Aᵀ) = ker f`, `J` arbitrary (07Z8, 07Z9).
- `Module.fittingIdeal_mono`, `Module.fittingIdeal_le_succ`: `Fitt_k ⊆ Fitt_{k+1}` (07Z9).
- `Module.fittingIdeal_eq_top_of_span_eq_top` (`[Finite ι]`): generated by `v : ι → M` with
  `Nat.card ι ≤ k` gives `Fitt_k = ⊤` (07ZA (1)).
- `Module.fittingIdeal_le_of_surjective` (07ZA (5)), `Module.fittingIdeal_congr`.
- `Module.fittingIdeal_zero_le_annihilator`: `Fitt_0 ≤ annihilator` (07ZA (6));
  `Module.annihilator_pow_le_fittingIdeal_zero`: `annihilator ^ n ≤ Fitt_0` for `n`
  generators.
- `Module.fittingIdeal_zero_quotient`: `Fitt_0(R ⧸ I) = I` (07ZA (8)).
- `Module.fittingIdeal_pi`, `Module.fittingIdeal_of_basis` (`[Finite ι]`):
  `Fitt_k = if Nat.card ι ≤ k then ⊤ else ⊥` for `ι → R` and for a module with a finite basis
  indexed by `ι` (07ZB).

[BaseChange](BaseChange.lean):

- `Module.fittingIdeal_baseChange` (`[Module.Finite R M]`, `[Algebra R S]`):
  `fittingIdeal S (S ⊗[R] M) k = (fittingIdeal R M k).map (algebraMap R S)` (07ZA (3)).
- `Module.fittingIdeal_of_isBaseChange`, `Module.fittingIdeal_of_isLocalizedModule`
  (`[Module.Finite R M]`).

[LocallyFree](LocallyFree.lean):

- `Module.span_compl_range_eq_top_of_isUnit_det` (`[Finite ι]`): a unit minor of relations at
  the rows of an injective `e : Fin s → ι` leaves the generators outside `Set.range e`
  generating `M`.
- `Module.fittingIdeal_eq_top_iff_exists_finset` (`[IsLocalRing R]`, `[Module.Finite R M]`):
  `Fitt_k = ⊤ ↔ ∃ s : Finset M, s.card ≤ k ∧ span R s = ⊤` (07ZC (1) ⇔ (3) at the closed point).
- `Module.fittingIdeal_eq_bot_and_eq_top_iff_of_isLocalRing` (`[IsLocalRing R]`,
  `[Module.Finite R M]`): over a local ring,
  `((∀ k < r, Fitt_k = ⊥) ∧ Fitt_r = ⊤) ↔ Module.Free R M ∧ finrank R M = r`;
  `Module.free_and_finrank_eq_succ_iff_of_isLocalRing`: rank `r + 1` iff `Fitt_r = ⊥` and
  `Fitt_{r+1} = ⊤` (same hypotheses; 07ZD, local case).
- `Module.fittingIdeal_eq_bot_and_eq_top_iff` (`[Module.Finite R M]`):
  `((∀ k < r, Fitt_k = ⊥) ∧ Fitt_r = ⊤) ↔ ∀ p, p ∈ Module.freeLocus R M ∧
  Module.rankAtStalk M p = r`; `Module.fittingIdeal_eq_bot_and_eq_top_iff_succ` for rank
  `r + 1`; `Module.fittingIdeal_eq_bot_and_eq_top_iff_projective`
  (`[Module.FinitePresentation R M]`): `↔ Module.Projective R M ∧ ∀ p, rankAtStalk M p = r`
  (07ZD).
- These prove 07ZD with (1) in stalkwise form: free of rank `r` at every prime, not the Zariski
  cover by `D(f_i)` with `M_{f_i} ≅ R_{f_i}^r` of Stacks' Definition 10.78.1. The passage to the
  Zariski-local form goes through 07ZC (4), which is not formalized; for the same reason the
  projective form assumes finite presentation, which Stacks does not need.

[Stratification](Stratification.lean):

- `Module.fittingIdeal_eq_top_iff_finrank_le` (`[Module.Finite K V]`): over a field,
  `Fitt_k(V) = ⊤ ↔ finrank ≤ k`.
- `Module.finrank_residueField_tensor_le_iff` (`[Module.Finite R M]`, `p` prime):
  `finrank κ(p) (κ(p) ⊗[R] M) ≤ k ↔ ¬ Fitt_k ≤ p` (07ZC (1) ⇔ (2)).
- `Module.setOf_finrank_residueField_tensor_eq_succ` (`[Module.Finite R M]`):
  `{p | finrank κ(p) (κ(p) ⊗[R] M) = r + 1} = zeroLocus Fitt_r \ zeroLocus Fitt_{r+1}`;
  `Module.setOf_finrank_residueField_tensor_eq_zero` (same hypothesis): the rank-`0` locus is
  `(zeroLocus Fitt_0)ᶜ`.
- `Module.fittingIdeal_map_eq_bot_and_eq_top_iff` (`[Module.Finite R M]`): for a ring map
  `R → S`, all stalks of `S ⊗[R] M` are free of rank `r` iff `Fitt_k(M) S = ⊥` for `k < r` and
  `Fitt_r(M) S = ⊤`. This is the algebraic content of the universal property of the Fitting
  stratum `V(Fitt_{r-1}) ∩ D(Fitt_r)`. Nitsure's own proof of the case `n = 0` of Theorem 4.3
  (pp. 19–20) uses a local minimal presentation instead of Fitting ideals; the Fitting stratum is
  the global form with the same universal property. The factorization of `Spec S → Spec R`
  through a scheme-level stratum is not part of this family.

The Stacks convention `Fitt_{-1} = 0` is encoded by the hypothesis `∀ k < r, Fitt_k = ⊥`
(vacuous for `r = 0`). The fibre-rank stratum is not the free locus: for `R = k[x]/(x²)` and
`M = R/(x)`, the prime lies in `V(Fitt_0) \ V(Fitt_1)` while `M_p` is not free.

## Proof route

Independence (07Z8) does not use Cauchy–Binet. The kernel of a presentation with one extra
generator `y = f a` is spanned by the old kernel, extended by zero, and the relation
`single none 1 - E a`; by multilinearity of minors (`span_minorIdeal`) and
expansion along that relation row (`det_succ_row`) or along the new coordinate
(`det_succ_column`), the minor ideal of size `r + 1` of the new kernel is the minor ideal of size
`r` of the old one. Finitely many generators are added by `Finite.induction_empty_option`, and
two presentations are compared through their sum. Base change uses right exactness
(`lTensor_exact`) and `RingHom.map_det`. The local criterion extracts a unit minor (local ring),
eliminates the corresponding generators with the inverse matrix, and kills the kernel with
`Fitt_{r-1} = ⊥ = I_1(ker)`.
