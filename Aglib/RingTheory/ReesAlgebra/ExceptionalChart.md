# The actual exceptional saturated chart

For the original ideal `I` in a commutative ring `A` and an actual element
`a : I`, write `S` for the saturated chart `A[I/a]`.
`exceptionalChartEquiv` constructs

```
S / (image a) ≃ Away (associatedGradedGrading I) (image (aX)).
```

The target uses the actual associated graded ring, defined as the original
Rees algebra modulo the ideal extended from `I`. Rees dehomogenization
retains the original coefficient map. The extended centre ideal becomes
the principal ideal generated by the original exceptional element, and
homogeneous quotient compatibility gives the equivalence.

The concrete construction carries the original target grading through one
generic quotient comparison. Its degree-one membership and numerator law
use that same grading throughout; the comparison does not require a second
grading-instance identification.

The coefficient formula preserves each original scalar. The fraction
formula sends the original chart element `b/a` to the homogeneous fraction
of `image (bX)` by `image (aX)`. These formulas retain the maps needed by
the generic exceptional-point and corrected Case-II arguments.

The construction applies to arbitrary commutative rings and ideals,
including degenerate denominators. It has no flatness, domain, Noetherian,
normality or tangent-equation premise. In particular it never replaces the
Rees special fibre by the Rees algebra of the zero extended ideal.
Identifying the actual associated graded ring with its tangent-square
model, and deriving normality of the geometric blowup, remain separate
obligations for the resolution consumer.
