# Generic freeness

Import `Aglib.RingTheory.GenericFreeness.Noetherian` for generic freeness over a
Noetherian domain (Stacks Project, Tag 051R, Lemma 10.118.1; Nitsure, *Construction of
Hilbert and Quot schemes*, arXiv:math/0504590v1, Lemma 4.1, p. 18).

`Module.Finite.exists_ne_zero_free_localizedModule_away`: let `A` be a Noetherian domain,
`B` an `A`-algebra with `Algebra.FiniteType A B`, and `M` a `B`-module with
`Module.Finite B M`, carrying its given `A`-module structure with `IsScalarTower A B M`.
Then there is `a : A`, `a ≠ 0`, such that `LocalizedModule.Away a M`
(`= LocalizedModule (Submonoid.powers a) M`) is free over `Localization.Away a`.
`Module.Finite.exists_ne_zero_flat_localizedModule_away` records the flat consequence, and
`Module.Finite.exists_free_localizedModule_away_of_isNoetherianRing` is the case of a
finite `A`-module, obtained from Mathlib's
`Module.FinitePresentation.exists_free_localizedModule_powers` at the generic point.

The sources prove generic freeness by Noether normalization and induction on the dimension
of the generic fibre. The proof here inducts on the number of polynomial variables instead.
For `B` generated by one element `x` over a Noetherian `A`-algebra `C` satisfying the
statement, the `C`-span `M₀` of finitely many `B`-generators of `M` defines the exhaustive
filtration `F 0 = ⊥`, `F (j + 1) = M₀ + x F j` by finite `C`-modules. Multiplication by `x`
maps each graded piece onto the next; Noetherianity of `C` makes these maps eventually
bijective, so finitely many denominators suffice. The power-filtration construction and
the induction predicate are private to the leaf.

[Filtration](Filtration.lean) holds the linear algebra, for arbitrary commutative rings:

- `Module.free_of_filtration`: an exhaustive increasing `ℕ`-filtration with `F 0 = ⊥` and
  free graded pieces `↥(F (j + 1)) ⧸ (F j).comap (F (j + 1)).subtype` gives a free module;
- `Module.free_localizedModule_of_filtration` and its variant
  `Module.free_localizedModule_of_filtration_of_isScalarTower` (filtrations by submodules
  over an algebra `C`): free localized graded pieces give a free localization;
- `Module.free_of_isLocalizedModule_of_free_isLocalizedModule`,
  `Module.free_localizedModule_of_linearEquiv` and
  `Module.free_localizedModule_away_of_dvd`: freeness of a localization is independent of
  the model, transported by linear equivalences, and passes from `M[1/a]` to `M[1/b]` when
  `a ∣ b`.

[Localization](Localization.lean) passes to localizations of the algebra:
`Module.Flat.of_isLocalizedModule_of_isScalarTower` (flatness over `R` survives localization
at a submonoid of an `R`-algebra), `Module.flat_of_isLocalizedModule_of_flat_localizedModule_away`
(if `M[1/a]` is flat over `A[1/a]`, every localization of the `B`-module `M` at a submonoid
containing the image of `a` is `A`-flat), and
`Module.Finite.exists_ne_zero_flat_localizedModule_primeCompl` (under the generic freeness
hypotheses, `M_P` is `A`-flat for every prime `P` of `B` not containing the image of some
`a ≠ 0`).

The scheme-level consumer is generic flatness in
[Modules/Flat/Generic](../../AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Flat/Generic/README.md), the first
half of plan row F12 (flattening stratification, Nitsure Thm 4.3, is a later slice).
The focused tests are `AglibTest.RingTheory.GenericFreeness.Filtration`,
`AglibTest.RingTheory.GenericFreeness.Noetherian` and
`AglibTest.RingTheory.GenericFreeness.Localization`; the inspected public proofs use only
`propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.
