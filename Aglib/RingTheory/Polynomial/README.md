# Polynomial relations and finite chart algebras

`GraphRelations` proves the nonzero cleared-denominator relation, constructs
an actual relation from the displayed injective fraction-field comparison, and
bounds the dimension of a supplied surjective polynomial quotient. Projectivity
of the finite polynomial algebra retains its domain and faithful scalar-map
conditions. These results are generic inputs to downstream chart arguments.

[CoefficientProducts](CoefficientProducts.md) contracts a nonzero polynomial
multiple through an injective integral algebra map and proves cross-product
integrality of coefficients in the original common field. Its normal-local
fraction-field endpoint selects an original coefficient generating the local
row from the stated product/content equation. No separability or finite-free
basis is imposed. The coefficient-ideal finite-domination construction
consumes these literal polynomial identities.
