# Representable constructions on sites

[LiftedYoneda](LiftedYoneda.lean) gives Hom and isomorphism constructions on a
full test subcategory, including represented presheaves with supplied sheafhood.
[BinaryOperation](BinaryOperation.lean) recovers an actual operation from its
original product test. [GroupHom](GroupHom.lean) uses the restricted Yoneda
inverse and the stated test, terminal and product comparisons to obtain the
whole Hom equivalence for represented commutative groups.
