# Generic descent: transfer, composition and sheaf conditions over `Over S`

Seven leaves extend Mathlib's effective-descent predicate `Pseudofunctor.IsStackFor` for
pseudofunctors `LocallyDiscrete Cᵒᵖ ⥤ᵖ Cat`, for arbitrary categories, functors and morphisms.
[Transfer](Transfer.lean) and [SiteChange](SiteChange.lean) transfer it between pseudofunctors;
they are instantiated once, in
[AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/Modules/Fppf](../../../AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/Modules/Fppf/README.md),
at `Spec : CommRingCatᵒᵖ ⥤ Scheme`. [Bind](Bind.lean) and [Composition](Composition.lean) give
effective descent along composites of covering families and presieves, for prestacks, and
monotonicity in the topology. [OverSigma](OverSigma.lean) turns sheaf conditions for presheaves on
`Over S` into sheaf conditions for presheaves on `C`. The last three are used by the fpqc and fppf
stacks of the same Fppf subfamily. [OverForget](OverForget.lean) transfers stacks to
`Over S₀` along the forgetful functor, and [Rigidification](Rigidification.lean) gives the
pullback coherence of rigidifications along a transformation; both are used by the Picard
comparison family ([Picard/Comparison](../../../AlgebraicGeometry/Picard/Comparison/README.md)).

[Transfer](Transfer.lean): a `Pseudofunctor.CompTrans F₁ F₂` consists of functors
`app X : F₁.obj ⟨op X⟩ ⥤ F₂.obj ⟨op X⟩`, pseudonaturality isomorphisms
`nat f : F₁.map f ⋙ app Y ≅ app X ⋙ F₂.map f` and their compatibility with the composition
isomorphisms `mapComp'` (`nat_comp`, componentwise): the data and composition coherence of a
strong transformation; the unit coherence is omitted because descent data do not use it.

- `CompTrans.conj`: conjugation of a descent morphism by `nat`; `conj_id`, `conj_comp`,
  `map_comp_conj`, `unconj`, `conj_injective`;
- `CompTrans.pullHom_conj`: conjugation commutes with Mathlib's `pullHom`;
- `CompTrans.descentData : F₁.DescentData f ⥤ F₂.DescentData f`, the square
  `toDescentDataIso : F₁.toDescentData f ⋙ η.descentData f ≅ η.app S ⋙ F₂.toDescentData f`,
  `fullyFaithfulDescentData`, `essSurj_descentData`, `isEquivalence_descentData`,
  `isEquivalence_toDescentData`;
- `CompTrans.isStackFor_of_isStackFor`: if every `app X` is an equivalence, `F₂` is a stack for
  every presieve for which `F₁` is;
- `CompTrans.exists_descentData_iso_of_bijective`: a descent datum for `F₂` whose objects are
  images under `η.app` of objects in a pullback-stable class lifts along `η`, when `η.app Y` is
  bijective on morphisms with target in that class (`essSurj_descentData` with weakened
  hypotheses);
- `DescentData.transport` (a descent datum transported along isomorphisms of its objects) and
  `LocallyDiscreteOpToCat.pullHom_map_comp_comp_map` (`pullHom` of a framed morphism); their
  intended owner is Mathlib's `Sites/Descent/DescentData.lean`.

[SiteChange](SiteChange.lean): for `G : C ⥤ D` and `F : LocallyDiscrete Dᵒᵖ ⥤ᵖ Cat`,
`F.restrictAlong G := G.op.toPseudofunctor.comp F`.

- `restrictAlong_mapComp'_hom_app`, `restrictAlong_pullHom`, `restrictAlong_pullHom'`: the
  composition isomorphisms and `pullHom` of the restriction are those of `F` at the images;
- `ChosenPullback.map`, `ChosenPullback₃.map`: images of chosen pullbacks under a functor that
  maps the squares to pullbacks (reducible, so that the projections are `G.map` of the original
  ones); their intended owner is Mathlib's `Limits/Shapes/Pullback/ChosenPullback.lean`
  (`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/ChosenPullback.lean`), next to the structures
  `ChosenPullback` and `ChosenPullback₃`;
- `pullHom'_self_restrictAlong_iff`, `pullHom'_comp_restrictAlong_iff`: the conditions of
  Mathlib's `DescentData'` transfer; `restrictDescentData'Equivalence`: descent data for
  `F.restrictAlong G` relative to `f` are descent data for `F` relative to `G.map ∘ f`;
- `toDescentDataRestrictAlongIso`, `isEquivalence_toDescentData_of_restrictAlong`,
  `isStackFor_ofArrows_map_of_restrictAlong`: if `F.restrictAlong G` is a stack for the presieve
  generated by `f`, then `F` is a stack for the presieve generated by the image family. The
  hypotheses are chosen pullbacks `sq i j : ChosenPullback (f i) (f j)` and chosen threefold
  pullbacks `sq₃ i₁ i₂ i₃ : ChosenPullback₃ (sq i₁ i₂) (sq i₂ i₃) (sq i₁ i₃)` in `C`, such that
  `G` maps every square of `sq` to a pullback square (`hsq i j : IsPullback (G.map (sq i j).p₁)
  (G.map (sq i j).p₂) (G.map (f i)) (G.map (f j))`) and the inner square of every `sq₃` to a
  pullback square (`hsq₃ i₁ i₂ i₃ : IsPullback (G.map (sq₃ i₁ i₂ i₃).chosenPullback.p₁)
  (G.map (sq₃ i₁ i₂ i₃).chosenPullback.p₂) (G.map (sq i₁ i₂).p₂) (G.map (sq i₂ i₃).p₁))`). No
  other hypothesis on `G` or `F` is needed; in particular `C` need not have pullbacks and `G`
  need not preserve them;
- `mapComp'_paste_app`, `CompTrans.ofNatTransNat`, `CompTrans.ofNatTrans F τ`: a natural
  transformation `τ : G₁ ⟶ G₂` of functors `C ⥤ D` induces the transformation
  `F.restrictAlong G₂ ⟶ F.restrictAlong G₁` with components `F.map (τ.app X)`; its `nat_comp` is
  a pasting of Mathlib's `mapComp'` associativity lemmas;
- `CompTransAlong G F F₁`: a `CompTrans F₁ (F.restrictAlong G)` stated with `F` and `G.map`
  directly (`CompTransAlong.toCompTrans`); `NatCompAlong G F F₁ app nat f g gf h M`, the
  proposition of its field `nat_comp` as a definition, and the constructor
  `CompTransAlong.mk'` taking the field in that form. In development, stating the field
  through `NatCompAlong` is what made the field of `specCompTransAlong` at `G = Spec` checkable
  by the kernel. Working hypothesis
  (not checked against the kernel implementation): the kernel first compares the arguments of
  two applications of a definition such as `NatCompAlong`, and so need not unfold the
  composites of the field.

Transfer and SiteChange use `set_option backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false` (and
`backward.defeqAttrib.useBackward true`) on the declarations whose goals must see the objects of
`F.obj _` (a `Cat.of`) as objects of the underlying category, as Mathlib's descent files do. Bind
uses `backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false` on three declarations (`pullHom_conj`,
`exists_pullHom_eq`, `CompDescent.pullHom_comparisonIso_hom`), each with an adjacent comment: the
`mapComp'` and `pullHom` coherence lemmas of Mathlib are stated with objects that agree with those
of the goals only up to unfolding. Composition and OverSigma use no options.
Implicit objects of composites are normalized with `Functor.comp_obj` inside `simp only` sets;
cancellations of isomorphism components are done in term mode (`cancel_epi`, `cancel_mono`).

[Bind](Bind.lean): effective descent along composites of families, for a `J`-prestack `F`.

- `LocallyDiscreteOpToCat.pullHom_comp`, `LocallyDiscreteOpToCat.pullHom_id'`: `pullHom` of a
  composite and of an identity morphism (the prime: Mathlib's `pullHom_id` is `pullHom` along an
  identity); `LocallyDiscreteOpToCat.pullHom_congr_left`: `pullHom` along equal morphisms;
- `toDescentData_comm_iff`: the compatibility of a family of morphisms between the descent data of
  two objects is the equality of their `pullHom`s; intended owner Mathlib's
  `Sites/Descent/DescentData.lean`;
- `eq_of_pullHom_eq`, `exists_pullHom_eq`: morphisms between pullbacks over `W` are determined
  by, and glue from, compatible families of their `pullHom`s along a covering sieve of `W`;
- `DescentData.restrictComp` (the restrictions along the families `g i : Y i j ⟶ X i` of a
  descent datum `E` along the composite family `g i j ≫ f i`) and `DescentData.hom_congr_base`;
- the construction, in the namespace `Pseudofunctor.CompDescent`: `descendedObj` (the object over
  `X i` descended from `E` along `g i`), `objIso` and its components `objIsoHom`, `objIsoInv`,
  `objIsoApp` (stated with explicit source and target, see below), `comparisonIso` (the
  comparison of `descendedObj` with `E` over an arrow through `g i j`), `localHom` (the descended
  morphism over a test object, before gluing), `factorSieve` (the covering sieve of the arrows
  along which two test morphisms factor through the families `g i₁`, `g i₂`), `chosenLocalHom`,
  `gluedHom` (the glued descent morphisms), `gluedDescentData` (a descent datum along `f`) and
  `pullIso` (its pullback to the composite family is isomorphic to `E`);
- `isEquivalence_toDescentData_comp`: if `f` and every `g i` are `J`-covering and
  `F.toDescentData f`, `F.toDescentData (g i)` are equivalences, so is `F.toDescentData` of the
  composite family.

Development observation: the component `(objIso f g E i).hom.hom j` has source
`((F.toDescentData (g i)).obj _).obj j` and target `(E.restrictComp f g i).obj j`, which agree
with `(F.map (g i j).op.toLoc).toFunctor.obj _` and `E.obj ⟨i, j⟩` only after unfolding; rewriting
with `Category.comp_id` and `Functor.map_comp` then failed to match. Restating the components
through definitions with the explicit source and target (`objIsoHom`, `objIsoInv`), and the
`comm` goal of `pullIso` as a lemma with explicit types (`pullIso_comm`), removed the failures;
the module builds in about 6 seconds.

[Composition](Composition.lean): `Pseudofunctor.IsPrestack.of_le`, `Pseudofunctor.IsStack.of_le`
(a (pre)stack for a finer topology is one for a coarser topology), and
`Pseudofunctor.IsStackFor.bind`: for a `J`-prestack `F`, a `J`-covering presieve `R` for which `F`
is a stack and `J`-covering presieves `R' f` for which `F` is a stack, `F` is a stack for
`R.bind R'` (the presieve form of `isEquivalence_toDescentData_comp`, for families indexed by
`R.category`). Consumers: [Fppf/Prestack](../../../AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/Modules/Fppf/Prestack.lean)
(`IsPrestack.of_le`) and [Fppf/Stack](../../../AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/Modules/Fppf/Stack.lean).
A locator for the composition statement for stacks is `citation needed` (Giraud, *Cohomologie non
abélienne*, Chapter II, expected, not inspected).

[OverSigma](OverSigma.lean): `Presieve.overSigma P`, the presheaf on `C` of pairs of a morphism
`g : Y ⟶ S` and a section of a presheaf `P` on `Over S` over `Over.mk g`; `overSigma_map_apply`;
`isSheafFor_of_isSheafFor_overSigma` (a sheaf condition for `overSigma P` gives one for `P` at
`Over.mk (𝟙 S)` when `yoneda.obj S` is separated) and `isSheafFor_overSigma` (the converse
direction, sieve by sieve). The helpers about restriction along morphisms of `Over S` are private.
Consumer: [Fppf/Prestack](../../../AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/Modules/Fppf/Prestack.lean), where
Mathlib's `isSheaf_type_propQCTopology_iff` is applied to `overSigma (F.presheafHom M N)`.

Intended Mathlib owners of the last three leaves: `IsPrestack.of_le`, `IsStack.of_le`,
`IsStackFor.bind` and `isEquivalence_toDescentData_comp` next to `IsStackFor.of_le` in
`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Descent/Precoverage.lean`; `pullHom_comp`, `eq_of_pullHom_eq` and
`exists_pullHom_eq` next to `pullHom` in `Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Descent/IsPrestack.lean`;
`DescentData.hom_congr_base` in `Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Descent/DescentData.lean`; and the
`overSigma` correspondence in `Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Sites/Over.lean`.

Focused consumer:
[AglibTest/CategoryTheory/Sites/Descent/Composition.lean](../../../../AglibTest/CategoryTheory/Sites/Descent/Composition.lean)
(axiom guards).

[OverForget](OverForget.lean): for a family `g i : U i ⟶ T.left` with `T : Over S₀`, the family
`Over.homMkFamily g` of `Over S₀` (the morphisms `Over.homMk (g i)`), with the helpers
`Over.homMkOf`, `Over.homMkLeft`, `Over.homMkOfComp`, `Over.homMkLift` and `Over.homMkMap` for
morphisms of `Over S₀` with prescribed underlying morphisms.

- `DescentData.hom_eq_hom_overMk`: a descent datum for `F.restrictAlong (Over.forget S₀)` along
  `Over.homMkFamily g` depends only on the underlying morphisms;
- `DescentData.ofOver`, the fully faithful functor `DescentData.ofOverFunctor` and
  `DescentData.toDescentDataOfOverIso`: such data are descent data for `F` along `g`, compatibly
  with `toDescentData`;
- `isStackFor_restrictAlong_overForget`: if `F` is a stack for the presieve of `g`, then
  `F.restrictAlong (Over.forget S₀)` is a stack for the presieve of `Over.homMkFamily g` (the
  converse direction of `isStackFor_ofArrows_map_of_restrictAlong`). Consumer:
  `Picard/Comparison/LocallyTrivial.lean`.

[Rigidification](Rigidification.lean): for `η : CompTrans F₁ F₂`, objects `E Y` of `F₂` and
morphisms `e y : y^* (E V) ⟶ E Y`:

- `CompTrans.pullRig y α : η_Y (y^* M) ⟶ E Y`, the rigidification of a pulled-back object, for
  `α : η_V M ⟶ E V`;
- `pullRig_comp` and `rigCompat_pullHom`, under the hypothesis that the `e y` are compatible with
  the composition isomorphisms of `F₂`: the rigidification along a composite, and the stability
  of the compatibility `CompTrans.RigCompat` under Mathlib's `pullHom`;
- `CompEq f g h := f ≫ g = h` (namespace `CategoryTheory`) and `CompEq.eq`: a regular definition,
  so that two instances, possibly in different presentations of one category, are compared
  argument by argument before unfolding. Consumers: `Picard/Comparison/{Gluing,GluingDescent}.lean`.
