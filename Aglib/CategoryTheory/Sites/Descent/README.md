# Generic descent transfer

Two leaves transfer Mathlib's effective-descent predicate `Pseudofunctor.IsStackFor` between
pseudofunctors `LocallyDiscrete Cᵒᵖ ⥤ᵖ Cat`, for arbitrary categories, functors and morphisms.
They are instantiated once, in
[AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/Modules/Fppf](../../../AlgebraicGeometry/Descent/Modules/Fppf/README.md),
at `Spec : CommRingCatᵒᵖ ⥤ Scheme`.

[Transfer](Transfer.lean): a `Pseudofunctor.CompTrans F₁ F₂` consists of functors
`app X : F₁.obj ⟨op X⟩ ⥤ F₂.obj ⟨op X⟩`, pseudonaturality isomorphisms
`nat f : F₁.map f ⋙ app Y ≅ app X ⋙ F₂.map f` and their compatibility with the composition
isomorphisms `mapComp'` (`nat_comp`, componentwise): the data and composition coherence of a
strong transformation; the unit coherence is omitted because descent data do not use it.

- `CompTrans.conj`: conjugation of a descent morphism by `nat`; `conj_id`, `conj_comp`,
  `map_comp_conj`, `unconj`, `conj_injective`;
- `CompTrans.pullHom_conj`: conjugation commutes with Mathlib's `pullHom`;
- `CompTrans.descentData : F₁.DescentData f ⥤ F₂.DescentData f`, the square
  `toDescentDataIso : F₁.toDescentData f ⋙ η.descentData f ≅ η.app S ⋙ F₂.toDescentData f`,
  `fullyFaithfulDescentData`, `essSurj_descentData`, `isEquivalence_descentData`,
  `isEquivalence_toDescentData`;
- `CompTrans.isStackFor_of_isStackFor`: if every `app X` is an equivalence, `F₂` is a stack for
  every presieve for which `F₁` is;
- `DescentData.transport` (a descent datum transported along isomorphisms of its objects) and
  `LocallyDiscreteOpToCat.pullHom_map_comp_comp_map` (`pullHom` of a framed morphism); their
  intended owner is Mathlib's `Sites/Descent/DescentData.lean`.

[SiteChange](SiteChange.lean): for `G : C ⥤ D` and `F : LocallyDiscrete Dᵒᵖ ⥤ᵖ Cat`,
`F.restrictAlong G := G.op.toPseudofunctor.comp F`.

- `restrictAlong_mapComp'_hom_app`, `restrictAlong_pullHom`, `restrictAlong_pullHom'`: the
  composition isomorphisms and `pullHom` of the restriction are those of `F` at the images;
- `ChosenPullback.map`, `ChosenPullback₃.map`: images of chosen pullbacks under a functor that
  maps the squares to pullbacks (reducible, so that the projections are `G.map` of the original
  ones); their intended owner is Mathlib's `Limits/Shapes/Pullback/ChosenPullback.lean`
  (`Mathlib/CategoryTheory/Limits/Shapes/Pullback/ChosenPullback.lean`), next to the structures
  `ChosenPullback` and `ChosenPullback₃`;
- `pullHom'_self_restrictAlong_iff`, `pullHom'_comp_restrictAlong_iff`: the conditions of
  Mathlib's `DescentData'` transfer; `restrictDescentData'Equivalence`: descent data for
  `F.restrictAlong G` relative to `f` are descent data for `F` relative to `G.map ∘ f`;
- `toDescentDataRestrictAlongIso`, `isEquivalence_toDescentData_of_restrictAlong`,
  `isStackFor_ofArrows_map_of_restrictAlong`: if `F.restrictAlong G` is a stack for the presieve
  generated by `f`, then `F` is a stack for the presieve generated by the image family. The
  hypotheses are chosen pullbacks `sq i j : ChosenPullback (f i) (f j)` and chosen threefold
  pullbacks `sq₃ i₁ i₂ i₃ : ChosenPullback₃ (sq i₁ i₂) (sq i₂ i₃) (sq i₁ i₃)` in `C`, such that
  `G` maps every square of `sq` to a pullback square (`hsq i j : IsPullback (G.map (sq i j).p₁)
  (G.map (sq i j).p₂) (G.map (f i)) (G.map (f j))`) and the inner square of every `sq₃` to a
  pullback square (`hsq₃ i₁ i₂ i₃ : IsPullback (G.map (sq₃ i₁ i₂ i₃).chosenPullback.p₁)
  (G.map (sq₃ i₁ i₂ i₃).chosenPullback.p₂) (G.map (sq i₁ i₂).p₂) (G.map (sq i₂ i₃).p₁))`). No
  other hypothesis on `G` or `F` is needed; in particular `C` need not have pullbacks and `G`
  need not preserve them;
- `CompTransAlong G F F₁`: a `CompTrans F₁ (F.restrictAlong G)` stated with `F` and `G.map`
  directly (`CompTransAlong.toCompTrans`); `NatCompAlong G F F₁ app nat f g gf h M`, the
  proposition of its field `nat_comp` as a definition, and the constructor
  `CompTransAlong.mk'` taking the field in that form. In development, stating the field
  through `NatCompAlong` is what made the field of `specCompTransAlong` at `G = Spec` checkable
  by the kernel. Working hypothesis
  (not checked against the kernel implementation): the kernel first compares the arguments of
  two applications of a definition such as `NatCompAlong`, and so need not unfold the
  composites of the field.

Both leaves use `set_option backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false` (and
`backward.defeqAttrib.useBackward true`) on the declarations whose goals must see the objects of
`F.obj _` (a `Cat.of`) as objects of the underlying category, as Mathlib's descent files do.
Implicit objects of composites are normalized with `Functor.comp_obj` inside `simp only` sets;
cancellations of isomorphism components are done in term mode (`cancel_epi`, `cancel_mono`).
