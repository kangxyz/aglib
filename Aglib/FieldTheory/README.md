# Field-theoretic prerequisites

The native owners are `AdjoinRoot`, `IntermediateField`, `Polynomial`,
`RatFunc`, `Algebra` and their actual maps. The selected leaves construct
root-field coefficient and branch comparisons, coprime splitting fields,
Sylow fixed-field embeddings and rational-function coefficient, inversion,
translation and reverse-polynomial maps.

They preserve irreducibility, normality, separability, finite-dimensional and
scalar-tower hypotheses. Milnor norm proofs consume these algebraic interfaces;
no Milnor quotient or motivic comparison is defined by this family.

[SeparablyGenerated/FiniteBasis](SeparablyGenerated/FiniteBasis.lean) extracts
a finite separating transcendence basis from an essentially finite-type,
separably generated field extension. Its cardinality is the actual
transcendence degree, and the extension over the field generated by that same
basis is finite and separable. The
[DVR source-coordinate construction](../RingTheory/DiscreteValuationRing/SourceSeparatingCoordinates.lean)
uses this basis rather than an assumed differential rank.

In prime exponential characteristic `p`,
[SeparablyGenerated/FrobeniusCriterion](SeparablyGenerated/FrobeniusCriterion.lean)
characterizes geometric reducedness by preservation of finite coefficient
independence over the literal Frobenius field ranges. The fields may have
independent universes; no finite imperfection degree is required.
[FrobeniusBaseEquiv](SeparablyGenerated/FrobeniusBaseEquiv.lean) transports the
condition along a specified base-field equivalence only with its actual
scalar-map equation. These are the field inputs to parameter-DVR completion
stability.
