# Localization of the original finite-source Ext map

[Localization.lean](Localization.lean) proves localization of the actual
native `Functor.mapExtLinearMap` when its source is finite over a
commutative Noetherian ring. The coefficient module is arbitrary. The
target ring is an actual localization along its specified algebra map;
flatness supplies the exactness of native scalar extension. No locality,
regularity, finite projective dimension, regular-sequence presentation,
coefficient finiteness or assumed Ext-localization result is a premise.
The ring and module carriers share the universe of the present native
scalar adjunction; its two supported Ext sizes remain independent.

The first public theorem, `extPostcomp_isLocalized_of_finite`, keeps the
base ring fixed. It proves that original Yoneda postcomposition by an
actual coefficient localization map is localizing in every degree.
Finite-presentation denominator lifting proves the degree-zero Hom case.
An actual finite free cover and its finite kernel give the original
degree-one Hom cokernel. A private lemma proves that the same map on
cokernel representatives localizes. In higher degrees, the original
unsigned Yoneda connecting map is a linear isomorphism because positive
Ext from the free middle term vanishes. Its coefficient naturality is
proved using original Yoneda composition.
On a Hom representative h, postcomposition by g sends h to `h ≫ g`,
meaning g after h.

`Module.exists_finite_presentation` supplies each actual finite free cover;
Noetherianity separately supplies finite kernels. Iterating these covers
gives the finite exact resolution segment needed in each degree. The proof
does not apply a local minimal-resolution theorem to a nonlocal ring and
does not assert a globally bounded finite free resolution over an arbitrary
Noetherian ring. These construction and cokernel helpers remain private.

`extScalarExtension_unit_equation` identifies the original exact-functor
Ext map under the actual scalar adjunction with postcomposition by that
same adjunction unit. The already established localization of the original
unit and the native exact-adjunction linear equivalence then give
`extScalarExtension_isLocalized_of_finite` for the literal
`F.mapExtLinearMap R M N n`. Thus the conclusion concerns the canonical
map itself. `extScalarExtension_precomp_endomorphism` retains original
source endomorphisms under the same scalar extension.

The current consumer is the actual affine closed-adjoint resolution for a
general finite quotient source. The published
`closedImmersionQuotientLocalizationHomologyMap_ext` identifies its
original principal restriction with this exact-functor Ext map, using
the original quotient and coefficient comparisons. Unlike the earlier
Koszul localization calculation, this result does not require the closed
ideal to be generated by a regular sequence. The geometric consumer must
still assemble affine sheaf descent, constant codimension, canonical-sheaf
concentration and the original trace normalization. No trace, GR, B7 or
surface resolution conclusion is asserted here.

The mathematical localization statement is a special case of
[Stacks, Lemma 15.67.4](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/087R).
The [finite-source closed-adjoint calculation](../../../../AlgebraicGeometry/Cohomology/ClosedImmersion/RightAdjoint/FiniteSource.md)
uses this original-map localization theorem.
