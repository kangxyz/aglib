# Original higher images above the surface fibre dimension

Source: [HigherDirectImage.lean](HigherDirectImage.lean).

For a proper dominant birational map of integral schemes with locally
Noetherian target and source dimension at most two, every original coherent
higher image vanishes in degrees above one. The original function-field
isomorphism and the source dimension bound are explicit hypotheses.

At a nongeneric point the fibre embeds in the proper closed preimage of the
point closure. At the generic point an actual isomorphism neighbourhood gives
acyclicity. The local step uses the original higher-image stalk comparison,
actual local-spectrum pullback and native closed-fibre isomorphism, then the
noncomplete local formal-functions vanishing theorem. No target stalk is
assumed complete or finite over the original base.
Consumers must derive R1 vanishing and the source dimension from their
actual rationality or maximal-model hypotheses.
