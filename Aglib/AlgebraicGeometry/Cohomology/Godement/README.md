# Actual Godement resolutions and derived comparisons

The owner is `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.Godement`. [First](First.lean) constructs the actual scalar stalk–skyscraper product functor and its unit, with the original point-projection maps. The first functor is exact on actual module-sheaf sequences. [Resolution](Resolution.lean) iterates the actual cokernel/unit sequence to form the resolution and augmentation.

[TotalComplex](TotalComplex/README.md) separates two scopes: categorical exactness on ordinary integer complex objects, and augmentation/quasi-isomorphism results under Plus membership or an explicit strict lower term bound. [Stalk](Stalk/README.md) supplies the actual splittings, signed matrix maps and bounded total homotopy. [DerivedPushforward](DerivedPushforward/README.md) computes the original D+ direct-image functor with its installed unit.

[Tensor](Tensor.lean) uses the canonical module tensor and bounded-below total unit. [ProjectionFormula](ProjectionFormula.lean) gives `godementProjectionFormulaMap_quasiIso` with finite locally free target coefficient, a bounded-below source complex and termwise flasqueness. [Contraction](Contraction.lean) defines the actual plain/resolved bounded-below functors and retains their lower-bound/termwise-flasque interfaces; it does not turn arbitrary additive data into a two-sided equivalence.

[Section](Section/README.md) records original section/total-unit comparisons. Actual derived projection formula, flat-base-change unit normalization and cohomological functoriality are consumers. These constructions do not supply the separate R5 `ProperModuleCohomologyFinite`, `SmoothDifferentialRank`, `LocalBasisFibreDimension` or `LocalFreeCohomologyBaseChange` families.

[PullbackPushforward](PullbackPushforward.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.Cohomology.Godement.PullbackPushforward`, constructs `Scheme.Modules.Godement.pullbackPushforwardPlus`: actual pullback, Godement totalization on the source scheme, then ordinary direct image. Its `pullbackPushforwardPlusUnit` goes from the identity on bounded-below target module complexes to this resolved composite, with the actual geometric and total units. These definitions require no flatness. The comparison `pullbackPushforwardPlusDerivedIso` with the actual derived composite retains flatness of the morphism and the canonical derived-category choices. `pullbackPushforwardPlusUnit_derived_comparison` identifies the installed units. `isIso_derivedUnit_of_quasiIso_unit` assumes the actual ordinary unit component is a quasi-isomorphism; `quasiIso_unit_of_isIso_derivedUnit` assumes the actual derived unit component is invertible. Neither direction supplies universal invertibility.

[OpenBaseChange](OpenBaseChange.lean) gives `Scheme.Modules.Godement.openPushPlusIso`, `openPullPlusIso` and `openTotalBridge` for a displayed square `a : W ⟶ U`, `b : W ⟶ V`, `f : U ⟶ X`, `j : V ⟶ X` with `IsPullback a b f j` and open immersions `a,j`. They use the actual module base-change maps and the original Godement total unit. `openBaseChangeGlobalLeg`, `openBaseChangeLocalLeg` and their quasi-isomorphism results use bounded-below flasque total terms. `pullbackPushforwardPlusUnit_restrict_quasiIso` retains the actual local unit quasi-isomorphism premise. No flatness of the remaining square maps, compactness or quasicoherence premise is added to this open descent statement. The span and component equations identify the actual global restricted unit, rather than an arbitrary isomorphic replacement.

This leaf's lower `Scheme.Modules.Plus.pushforward_quasiIso_of_flasque` applies to a quasi-isomorphism of bounded-below complexes whose source and target terms are flasque, along an arbitrary scheme morphism. `quasiIso_of_isIso_Q_map` detects the actual ordinary quasi-isomorphism through D+ localization; `quasiIso_of_span` retains an invertible middle comparison, the span equation and the three indicated quasi-isomorphism inputs. These statements do not assert ordinary direct-image preservation of every quasi-isomorphism.
