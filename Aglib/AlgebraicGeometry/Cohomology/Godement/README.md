# Actual Godement resolutions and derived comparisons

The owner is `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.Godement`. [First](First.lean) constructs the actual scalar stalk–skyscraper product functor and its unit, with the original point-projection maps. The first functor is exact on actual module-sheaf sequences. [Resolution](Resolution.lean) iterates the actual cokernel/unit sequence to form the resolution and augmentation.

[TotalComplex](TotalComplex/README.md) separates two scopes: categorical exactness on ordinary integer complex objects, and augmentation/quasi-isomorphism results under Plus membership or an explicit strict lower term bound. [Stalk](Stalk/README.md) supplies the actual splittings, signed matrix maps and bounded total homotopy. [DerivedPushforward](DerivedPushforward/README.md) computes the original D+ direct-image functor with its installed unit.

[Tensor](Tensor.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.Cohomology.Godement.Tensor`, defines `godementTensorUnit` by applying the canonical left tensor functor to the actual `godementTotalPlusUnit`. `godementTensorUnit_app` fixes that component. `godementTensorTotalUnit` further applies Godement totalization, and `godementTensorTotalUnit_app` retains the same mapped unit with its actual functor source and target. `godementTensorUnit_quasiIso` and `godementTensorTotalUnit_quasiIso` require the coefficient `E` to be finite locally free and the complex to lie in Plus. `godementTotalPlus_sameLowerBound` preserves a displayed strict lower term bound, and `godementTotalPlus_termwiseFlasque` supplies flasque terms of the actual total complex.

[ProjectionFormula](ProjectionFormula.lean) gives `godementProjectionFormulaMap_quasiIso` with finite locally free target coefficient, a bounded-below source complex and termwise flasqueness. [Contraction](Contraction.lean) constructs `godementContractionPlain` and `godementContractionResolved` for an additive endofunctor `F` and a coefficient `E`. A supplied isomorphism `e : F ⋙ moduleTensorLeftFunctor X E ≅ 𝟭 X.Modules` gives `godementContractionPlainIso` and the actual map `godementContractionMap`. `godementContractionMap_quasiIso` retains finite local freeness of `E`; `godementContractionMap_app` identifies the map as the inverse plain comparison followed by `godementContractionInsertion`. The resolved functor preserves a displayed strict lower term bound and has flasque terms. These are bounded-below, one-sided contraction interfaces.

[Section](Section/README.md) records original section/total-unit comparisons. Actual derived projection formula, flat-base-change unit normalization and cohomological functoriality are consumers. Proper finiteness is supplied separately by [Proper/Field](../Proper/Field/README.md); [smooth differential rank](../../Modules/Differentials/README.md) and [local-basis fibre dimension](../../Modules/LocallyFree/README.md) also have proved suppliers. The full `LocalFreeCohomologyBaseChange` family remains an explicit input; Godement construction alone does not supply it.

[PullbackPushforward](PullbackPushforward.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.Cohomology.Godement.PullbackPushforward`, constructs `Scheme.Modules.Godement.pullbackPushforwardPlus`: actual pullback, Godement totalization on the source scheme, then ordinary direct image. Its `pullbackPushforwardPlusUnit` goes from the identity on bounded-below target module complexes to this resolved composite, with the actual geometric and total units. These definitions require no flatness. The comparison `pullbackPushforwardPlusDerivedIso` with the actual derived composite retains flatness of the morphism and the canonical derived-category choices. `pullbackPushforwardPlusUnit_derived_comparison` identifies the installed units. `isIso_derivedUnit_of_quasiIso_unit` assumes the actual ordinary unit component is a quasi-isomorphism; `quasiIso_unit_of_isIso_derivedUnit` assumes the actual derived unit component is invertible. Neither direction supplies universal invertibility.

[The focused open-base-change guide](OpenBaseChange/README.md) records the cached construction and proof dependencies.

[OpenBaseChange](OpenBaseChange.lean) gives `Scheme.Modules.Godement.openPushPlusIso`, `openPullPlusIso` and `openTotalBridge` for a displayed square `a : W ⟶ U`, `b : W ⟶ V`, `f : U ⟶ X`, `j : V ⟶ X` with `IsPullback a b f j` and open immersions `a,j`. They use the actual module base-change maps and the original Godement total unit. `openBaseChangeGlobalLeg`, `openBaseChangeLocalLeg` and their quasi-isomorphism results use bounded-below flasque total terms. `pullbackPushforwardPlusUnit_restrict_quasiIso` retains the actual local unit quasi-isomorphism premise. No flatness of the remaining square maps, compactness or quasicoherence premise is added to this open descent statement. The equation `open_unit_span` identifies the actual restricted global unit, followed by `openPullbackPushforwardComparison` and the global leg, with the actual local unit followed by the local leg. `open_unit_span_component` and `open_unit_span_factorized` retain its termwise and functorial forms.

This leaf's lower `Scheme.Modules.Plus.pushforward_quasiIso_of_flasque` applies to a quasi-isomorphism of bounded-below complexes whose source and target terms are flasque, along an arbitrary scheme morphism. `quasiIso_of_isIso_Q_map` detects the actual ordinary quasi-isomorphism through D+ localization; `quasiIso_of_span` retains an invertible middle comparison, the span equation and the three indicated quasi-isomorphism inputs. These statements do not assert ordinary direct-image preservation of every quasi-isomorphism.
