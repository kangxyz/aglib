# ChartNonempty

Native owner: `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Blowup`.

The public declarations are `saturatedChart_nonempty`, `pairIdealChart_nonempty`.

They use the original objects and maps; relocation adds no mathematical premise or replacement realization.
