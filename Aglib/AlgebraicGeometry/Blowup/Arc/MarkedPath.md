# Formal arcs on the original marked ordinary point-blowup path

Import `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.Blowup.Arc.MarkedPath`.
The current consumer is the formal-arc construction for the corrected RDP
tail. The input is the actual given infinite ordinary closed-point blowup
path, with its original schemes, blowdowns and selected points.

`ClosedPointBlowupPath` records these original objects and the ordinary
Cartier and final-object universal properties of each displayed blowdown.
Its `datum` uses exactly the displayed source scheme and arrow. The points
are closed and map to the preceding original point. The starting scheme is
integral and locally Noetherian, with a nongeneric starting point. No arc,
DVR quotient, compatible function-field embeddings, or terminating chain
is included as an input.

The actual blowup integrality and generic-comparison provider derives
integrality, dominance, and nongeneric selected points at every stage.
The properness provider derives local Noetherianity of each original
scheme. `localRing` is its actual selected stalk, and `transition` is the
original blowdown stalk map with the explicit target-point stalk transport.
`scalar` composes these same actual local maps.

`stalkToFunctionField` is the canonical generic-specialization map from an
actual stalk into its scheme's function field. `stalkMap_functionField`
and `pointLocalRingMap_functionField` prove its naturality square for the
original scheme morphism and specified point. `stepFunctionFieldEquiv`
is the equivalence induced by the original blowdown's generic-point map;
its underlying ring-homomorphism equation is retained explicitly.

`functionFieldEquiv` composes those original equivalences. `embedding`
then puts every actual selected stalk into the same original function
field. Its injectivity follows from the native fraction-field structure.
`embedding_comp_transition` proves compatibility with the original local
transition, rather than assuming it. `embedding_scalar` retains the
original starting-stalk coefficients at every stage.

`rangeChain` uses Mathlib's native `LocalSubring.range`. `rangeMap` and
`rangeEquiv` retain the actual embedding and its literal range. The
proved embedding square shows that these ranges increase, and locality
of the original maps proves that they increase in the native domination
order, as stated by `range_monotone`.

`union` is the actual local-subring supremum constructed by the lower
`LocalRing.DirectedUnion` provider in the same original function field.
`unionMap` consists of the original stalk-to-range map followed by the
literal stage inclusion into that union. Its locality and original
transition equation are proved.

`Marking` keeps the original corrected-tail data: one parameter in the
starting stalk outside the square of its maximal ideal, bijectivity of
the original residue-field maps, and the actual exceptional-ideal map
equations using the original `scalar` images of that same parameter.
It assumes no persistent special cubic form.

The original range maps transport these actual exceptional-ideal equations
to the domination chain. `union_maximalIdeal` proves the union maximal ideal
is generated by the same original marker image, and
`union_marker_mem_nonZeroDivisors` proves that image is a nonzerodivisor in
the original function-field subring. `union_residue_bijective` retains the
actual starting-stalk residue comparison. The union is not assumed
Noetherian or separated.

`Marking.arcRing` is the one literal principal completion supplied by
`DirectedUnion`, with its native ring presentation fixed before constructing
the concrete maps. Its domain, DVR, local and complete structures are
derived from the published principal-completion provider. `stageMap` is the
original stalk-to-range map followed by the original union inclusion and
completion coefficient map. The original transition, fixed-parameter and
residue-field equations are proved, and every such map is local.

`Marking.arc` uses `stageMap` and the original `fromSpecStalk` map on each
given scheme. `arc_closedPoint` identifies its image with the original
selected point; `arc_map` retains the original blowdown triangle.
The actual image of the point-centre ideal is the principal uniformizer
ideal of the constructed DVR. `arc_cartier` proves its Cartier condition,
and `arc_eq_universalLift` identifies every next arc with the original
universal blowup lift. The uncompleted stage maps are not assumed surjective,
and these original uncompleted arcs are not asserted closed immersions.

`initialCompletion` is the literal maximal-adic completion of the original
starting stalk. `completionMap` uses the existing
`AdicCompletion.localParameterMap` provider to extend the actual stage-zero
map into the same complete DVR. The proved image of its maximal ideal is
the target maximal ideal, so its parameter-ideal radical meets that provider's
hypothesis. The target is Noetherian because it is the derived DVR; no
Noetherianity of the union is inserted. `completionMap_algebraMap` and
`completionMap_comp_algebraMap` preserve every original coefficient.

`completionMap_maximalIdeal` and the original residue comparison prove the
actual completed map is surjective. This uses the native complete-source,
Hausdorff-target surjectivity theorem; surjectivity is a conclusion.
The uniformizer lift remains the completion image of the same original
marker.

For the separate termination application, the source completion is explicitly
assumed to be a normal domain and to have dimension two. The actual kernel
has height one by the surface-surjection calculation. Normality and
the native one-dimensional normal-local criterion derive its generic DVR.
`completedState` uses exactly this constructed surjection and original
completed marker; `completedRealization` starts over `Spec` of that same
completion with identity local map. `exists_regular_completed_iterate`
applies the finite iteration at its same actual selected points.

`pointCentre_comap_of_preimmersion` retains the full actual point-centre
ideal under the localization projection. Its proof uses the Cartesian
square of the actual residue-field arrows. The completion adapter
`pointCentre_comap_completionResidueFiber` uses the original completion
residue isomorphism and the equality of the extended maximal ideal. They
make the literal closed-fibre square Cartesian; pasting the point square
then proves equality of the full point-centre ideals.

`localBaseChange` and `completedBaseChange` fix the two literal pullbacks
of the original path. The first projection is a flat preimmersion with
actual stalk isomorphisms. The completion projection is flat.
`regular_original_of_completed` uses pointwise C1 on the proper localized
structure map, followed by the localization stalk isomorphism, to transfer
regularity to the same original selected point.

`Marking.originalComparison` constructs the comparison from each constructed
completed iterate to the same original scheme. Its successor is the
canonical `BaseChange.map` for the full original point-centre ideal.
Flatness and the proved centre equality make its square Cartesian;
the original blowup universal property proves its arc triangle.
`originalComparison_blowdown` and `OriginalComparison.point` retain the
original blowdowns and selected points at every step.

`originalCompletedIso` is the induced canonical isomorphism to the literal
double base change. Its arc and point equations use both original
projections. `exists_regular_original_point` combines the finite
iteration, this constructed isomorphism, and the actual C1/localization
return. Its normal-domain and dimension-two conditions on the original
starting completion remain explicit. It concludes regularity at an
original selected point of the original given infinite marked path.

`exists_regular_original_point_of_complete_base` derives those completion
conditions from the original scalar map `A -> P.localRing 0`. It uses a
complete local domain `A` of dimension two, an injective local
scalar map with finite canonical residue extension, and normality, Nagata,
and dimension two of the original starting stalk. The
`AdicCompletion.normalDomain_of_complete_normal_surface` provider supplies
domainhood and integral closedness of its identical maximal-adic completion;
native dimension preservation supplies dimension two. The resulting theorem
has no normality or domainhood premise on the completion. Applying it to a
resolution tail still requires the actual original chart/scalar hypotheses.

`tail r` retains the literal original schemes, points and blowdowns starting
at index `r`. The original finite-prefix map is still `scalar r`.
`scalar_injective` uses its already proved common-field square.
`transition_essFiniteType` uses the original proper blowdown stalk map and
point transport; `scalar_essFiniteType` composes these original maps.
`scalar_isAlgebraic` puts the actual target stalk into the same original
function field as an algebra over the actual initial stalk, using
`embedding_scalar` for the coefficient equation.

`exists_regular_tail_of_complete_base` uses those results and the
`FiniteTypeSurface` essential-finite-type algebraic Nagata theorem to derive
Nagata of the original tail stalk from the complete original initial stalk.
It retains normality and dimension two of the actual tail stalk and an
actual marking on `tail r`. It also requires finiteness of the original
prefix residue map. `scalar_residue_finite_of_transition` can supply that
finiteness from the actual finite residue extensions along the prefix.

The unchanged-residue corollary instead derives it from explicit original
transition residue isomorphisms. An RDP application must supply those
isomorphisms for the actual prefix under its Case-II hypotheses. A marking
on the later tail alone supplies nothing about an earlier prefix. No
Case-I or arbitrary earlier residue extension is silently identified.

The union construction corresponds to the fixed source packet
`formal-arc-marked-path-union-v1.md`, SHA-256
`0b04e40f941770a234a93da7ced109b556fd52d7c1e632a42e33d1968fc21df3`.
It uses the existing principal-completion DVR theorem. Normality and
dimension of the original starting completion remain explicit in the
general termination interface; the complete-base corollaries derive them
under their stated original scalar and stalk hypotheses.
The [Arc.Iteration](Iteration.md) realization starts over the completed base;
completion projections are not asserted preimmersions or stalk isomorphisms.
No uncompleted arc is asserted a closed immersion, and no full R.D3, RDP
or global resolution theorem is concluded here.
