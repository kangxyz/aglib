# Relative forms on a two-generator ideal blowup

The declarations in `Scheme.Blowup.PairIdeal` use the original ideal
`I = (x,y)`, its canonical blowup and saturated charts.
[RawSource](RawSource.lean) compares the original raw relative exterior
forms on those charts. [OpenCentre](OpenCentre.lean) and
[OpenTransport](OpenTransport.lean) retain the actual open-centre ideal
equality, blowup projection and pullback maps.

For a domain `R`, a specified fraction field `K` with its compatible
`Λ → R → K` scalar tower, a proper ideal generated by a regular pair,
an exterior degree `n` and a map from the original degree-`n` forms to
a line `L`, [RawFamily](RawFamily.lean) and
[RawGluing](RawGluing.lean) glue the two one-pole chart maps uniquely.
The restriction and unit equations retain the original differential map
and the target `b*L ⊗ I_E^∨`, with the actual centre ideal `I_E`.

[Global](Global.lean) extends that same raw map to its bidual and proves
evaluation and unit compatibility.
[SourceRestriction](SourceRestriction.lean) and
[SourceEvaluation](SourceEvaluation.lean) compare the original bidual source
under the stated Noetherian and locally finite-type structural hypotheses.
These are regular-pair differential constructions; they do not assume
rationality or a resolution.

Present consumers include [Relative forms on saturated regular-pair charts](../RegularPair/README.md), [Universal blowups and saturated affine charts](../../README.md).
