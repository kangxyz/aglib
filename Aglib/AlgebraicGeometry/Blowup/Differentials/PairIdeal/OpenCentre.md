# Ordinary comparison of the original blowup centers

A displayed affine ideal and point of a scheme, together with the original center-comap equality, give the canonical comparison from the affine ideal blowup to the point blowup. An open immersion is required for its openness statement; the displayed closed-point condition supplies the actual point ideal line where needed.

The induced maps preserve the literal center schemes and ideal inclusions. The dual map's section equation uses the same native `InternalHom.transpose` of those ideal inclusions on the two original blowups. All maps and compatibility laws are ordinary scheme-module constructions, with the original parameters and hypotheses.
