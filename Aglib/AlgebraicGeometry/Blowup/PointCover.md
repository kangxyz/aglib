# Closed-point cover on the original canonical affine model

Source: [PointCover.lean](PointCover.lean).

`PointCover.lean`, in `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Blowup.PointCover`, constructs
one actual two-component open cover of a specified `BlowupDatum` for the
kernel ideal of the original residue-point map.

## Original objects and maps

Inputs are a scheme `X : Scheme.{u}`, a point `x`, its actual blowup datum `B`,
an affine open `V`, proof that `x` belongs to `V`, and closedness of `{x}`.
The first source is exactly

```
(blowupDatum (affineCenter Γ(X,V) ((X.fromSpecResidueField x).ker.ideal V))).Y.
```

`localMap` is the existing `BaseChange.map` along `V.2.fromSpec`, using the
proved exact centre equality `comap_fromSpec_eq_affineCenter`. Its constructor
formula, projection triangle, cartesian square, open immersion, and range
are exported. No affine-Proj representative, comparison isomorphism, or
replacement ideal generated by newly chosen elements is introduced.

The other source is the literal open `awayOpen := B.map ⁻¹ᵁ centreComplement I`,
with `I := (X.fromSpecResidueField x).ker`. Its arrow is the original open
inclusion. The original restricted structural map `B.map ∣_ centreComplement I`
is an isomorphism by the existing universal blowup law. `awayIso_hom` and
`awayIso_hom_ι` retain that map and its triangle over `X`.

Closedness gives `I.support = {x}` through the original point-map kernel,
its native range, and the genuine locally supplied closed-immersion instance.
Thus the two arrows cover `B.Y`. Native `Scheme.Cover.mkOfCovers` keeps the
first source literally canonical and the second source literally an open.
The index, both component objects and arrows, and both range formulas are
exported at this single constructor. No regularity, integrality, dimension,
Noetherianity, field or extra scalar action is required for this cover.

## Genuine nonempty and integral components

Under `[IsIntegral X]` and `ringKrullDim (X.presheaf.stalk x) = 2`, the existing
`ne_genericPoint_of_stalk_dimension_two` and original point-centre law place
`genericPoint X` outside the centre. The existing blowup lift gives an actual
preimage of that point. It lies in `awayOpen`; it also lies in the range of
`localMap`, because every nonempty affine neighbourhood contains the generic
point. This proves nonemptiness of both original sources. Existing integrality
of the same blowup and the original open immersions then give component
integrality. Nonemptiness is not inferred from closedness or `x ∈ V` alone.

The cover has no separately supplied field and imposes no identification of
field universes. Coordinate applications retain their own original field
and scalar data. They additionally require the regular-pair generators,
original raw coefficient/unit equations and the module-arrow comparison
on the off-centre open before applying the line-map gluing theorem.
The structural scheme isomorphism off the centre alone does not supply
that module-arrow equation.
