# Normal stalks, components and connected integrality

[Basic](Basic.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.Properties.Normal.Basic`, defines `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.NormalStalks X`: every actual stalk is a domain and integrally closed. `NormalStalks.isReduced` derives reducedness. `NormalComponentsOpen` records the component-openness conclusion on locally Noetherian schemes.

[IrreducibleComponents](../IrreducibleComponents.lean) proves the actual domain-stalk/component separation results. If every stalk is a domain, finitely many irreducible components imply that each component is open; a separate locally Noetherian theorem gives openness without global finiteness of the components. [Components](Components.lean) supplies `normalComponentsOpen` and `isIntegral_of_connected_normalStalks_actual`: a connected locally Noetherian scheme with normal stalks is integral. Connectedness is the native `ConnectedSpace` premise, including its nonempty scope.

The conditional `isIntegral_of_connected_normalStalks` takes the component-openness family separately; the supplier closes that interface on the same objects. [Smooth field normality](../../Morphisms/Smooth/README.md) supplies normal stalks from regular-local algebra. No properness or dimension-one premise is used for these general component results.

[Affine](Affine.lean) derives integral closedness of nonempty affine sections from integrality of the scheme and integral closedness of its actual stalks, without Noetherianity. It also proves that spectra of normal domains and spectra ring-isomorphic to finite products of normal domains have integrally closed domain stalks. The product proof uses actual factor open immersions and retains the empty-product case. These lower results feed [generic finite-etale normalization](../../Normalization/GenericFiniteEtale/README.md).
