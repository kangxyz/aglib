# The literal completed regular birational model

Import `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.Spec.CompletionLocalRing.RegularModel`.

`Scheme.LocalRing.completedRegularModel_properties` starts with a Noetherian
local normal domain `A` and a SUPPLIED integral regular proper birational
model `f : Y -> Spec A`, with its original function-field map invertible.
It proves that the literal maximal-adic completion is a normal domain and
that the LITERAL pullback of this same model is integral and regular. Its
original second projection is proper and dominant and has invertible
ORIGINAL function-field map.

The original normal-birational structure map supplies the actual structural
section bijection. The existing completion section comparison and the
single connected-normal proof give integrality before any completion-domain
assumption. The original proper-completion regularity theorem supplies
regularity, not arbitrary-flat regularity ascent.

A genuine isomorphism neighborhood of the original generic point pulls back
to a nonempty open along the actual faithfully flat completion map. The
native restriction square and original open-inclusion field maps prove
birationality of the literal second projection. All structural maps are the
same pullback projections and retain their native commuting square.

There is no dimension, Nagata, initial-completeness, absolute-separatedness,
completed-source integrality, completed-domain or completed-field-map
hypothesis. No resolution-existence theorem or replacement completed model
is introduced. This dimension-free geometry can feed the later surface
conversion only after its separate dimension and chain obligations are met.
