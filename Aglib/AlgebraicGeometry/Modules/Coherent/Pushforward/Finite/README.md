# Exact coherent direct image along finite morphisms

[GeneralPushforward](GeneralPushforward.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.Modules.Coherent.Pushforward.Finite.GeneralPushforward`, owns `Scheme.Modules.Coherent.Pushforward.Finite.coherentPushforward`, `coherentPushforwardExact` and `coherentOpenBaseChange`. The sole lower implementation is [QuasiCoherent/FiniteType/Pushforward](../../../QuasiCoherent/FiniteType/Pushforward.lean), on arbitrary finite morphisms and actual finite-type sheaves. `coherentPushforward` is its transparent alias typed on `Scheme.CoherentSheaf` and requires locally Noetherian source and target. `coherentPushforwardExact` has the same guard.

The transparent `Scheme.Modules.Coherent.Pushforward.Finite.Affine.coherentPushforward` and `coherentPushforwardExact` specializations are also defined in [GeneralPushforward](GeneralPushforward.lean). These guarded affine coherent aliases retain a finite affine ring map and Noetherian source/target rings. The generic affine finite-type alias remains below without those Noetherian classes. [AffinePushforward](AffinePushforward.lean) and [AffineFunctoriality](AffineFunctoriality.lean) expose their affine property, scalar and functoriality interfaces. The actual scalar-restriction square agrees with finite-module restriction on affine charts. Guarded `coherentOpenBaseChange` requires the original source/target local Noetherianity, finite map and open target map; local Noetherianity of that open source is derived, not an added caller premise. [Functoriality](Functoriality.lean) gives natural identity/composition comparisons and agreement with the actual closed-immersion construction.

Lower [finite-type affine comparison](../../../QuasiCoherent/Affine/README.md), canonical direct image and [finite scalar restriction](../../../../../Algebra/Category/ModuleCat/README.md) supply these interfaces. The general affine-cohomology pushforward result is separate and does not require the scheme morphism to be finite.
