# Strict and locally perfect module-sheaf complexes

This family concerns actual integer-indexed `CochainComplex X.Modules ℤ` on an arbitrary scheme. Its declarations live in `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.Complex`; focused imports have prefix `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.Modules.Perfect`. The lower interfaces are [ordinary complexes and geometric restriction](../Complex/README.md), [finite locally free sheaves](../LocallyFree/README.md) and [selected exact subcategories](../../../CategoryTheory/ExactCategory/README.md). No global quasi-compactness, Noetherianity or projectivity assumption is imposed.

[Strict](Strict.lean) defines `IsBounded K` by the existence of integers `a,b` such that `K.X n` is zero whenever `n < a` or `b < n`. These are bounds on the terms, rather than only on cohomology. `strictPerfect X K` adds finite local freeness of every term; `StrictPerfect X` is its full subcategory with ordinary chain maps. The family contains zero, is invariant under chain isomorphism, is closed under short exact extensions and finite products, and contains every finite locally free sheaf placed in degree zero by `single_strictPerfect` and `vectorBundleInclusion`. [Closure](Closure.lean) proves `strictPerfect_shift` and `strictPerfect_mappingCone` for actual shifts and the original signed mapping cone of a map between strict perfect complexes.

[Exact](Exact.lean) supplies `strictExactStructure`, inherited from the abelian category of all module-sheaf complexes. Its conflations are the actual ambient short exact sequences between strict perfect objects. `vectorBundleExactInclusion` makes the degree-zero inclusion an exact functor for the selected vector-bundle and strict-perfect exact structures, including sequences that split only locally. [Restriction](Restriction.lean) proves `strictPerfect_restrict` for any open immersion `j`, retaining the given term bounds, and packages the additive functor `strictRestrict` and exact functor `strictExactRestrict`.

[Local](Local.lean) defines `locallyPerfect X K`: for every point `x`, there is an open neighborhood `U` and `P : StrictPerfect U.toScheme` such that the images of `(restrict U.ι).obj K` and `P.obj` are isomorphic under the actual `DerivedCategory.Q`. The open and strict representative may vary with `x`. `Perfect X` is the full subcategory of ordinary complexes satisfying this predicate; its morphisms remain chain maps. It is not itself a derived-category localization. `locallyPerfect_of_strictPerfect` and `strictToPerfect` give the inclusion of strict perfect objects, and `strictToPerfect_comp_inclusion` keeps the underlying complex unchanged. `locallyPerfect_iff_of_quasiIso` proves invariance under an actual quasi-isomorphism. These interfaces do not supply a global strict representative for every locally perfect complex.

For an open immersion `j : U ⟶ X`, [LocalRestriction](LocalRestriction.lean) constructs `derivedRestrict j` from the exact geometric restriction functor, with its shift and triangulated structures. The files use Mathlib's standard native derived category, with morphisms in universe `u + 1` for `X : Scheme.{u}`. `derivedRestrictFactors j` is the canonical natural isomorphism

```lean
DerivedCategory.Q (C := X.Modules) ⋙ derivedRestrict j ≅
  restrict j ⋙ DerivedCategory.Q (C := U.Modules)
```

`restrictDerivedIso j e` transports a displayed derived isomorphism using this factorization's components at its two complexes, with the source component inverted. `locallyPerfect_iff_of_derivedIso` gives invariance under isomorphism of the actual localized objects. `locallyPerfect_restrict` and `perfectRestrict` preserve local perfectness along every open immersion; `restrictCompIso` fixes ordinary restriction under composition.

[LocalModels](LocalModels.lean) defines `HasStrictModel K` by a single strict perfect representative of `K` in the actual quasi-isomorphism localization over its whole scheme. Such a model restricts along open immersions and survives shrinking an open, by `HasStrictModel.restrict` and `HasStrictModel.restrict_of_le`. `exists_common_strictModels` gives one neighborhood of a chosen point on which two locally perfect complexes both have strict models. `locallyPerfect_of_openImmersion_models` accepts pointwise model witnesses on displayed open immersions and returns the neighborhood-based local-perfectness predicate. These results preserve the distinction between a local model, a global model and a term-bounded complex.

[Homology](Homology.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.Modules.Perfect.Homology`, proves `locallyPerfect_quasicoherent_homology`: every actual homology sheaf of a locally perfect complex is quasicoherent. `locallyPerfect_exists_local_homology_bounds` gives, at each point, an open neighborhood `U` and integers `a,b` such that the restriction of `K.homology i` to `U` is zero for `i < a` or `b < i`. These conclusions hold on arbitrary schemes, with no termwise quasicoherence assumption on `K` or global strict model. `locallyPerfect_homology_bounded` additionally assumes `CompactSpace X` and gives one such interval for the homology sheaves on all of `X`. This is a global bound on homology, not a term bound or a construction of a global strict representative. The proof uses the [ordinary homology comparisons](../Complex/Homology.lean), [quasicoherent homology](../QuasiCoherent/Homology.lean) and the actual local strict models.

[Affine](Affine.lean) proves `strictPerfect_tilde_of_finite_projective`:
native tilde of an actual strictly bounded finite-projective module complex
is `strictPerfect` on the original spectrum, with the same term bounds.
No Noetherianity or cohomology-only inference is used. Its focused actual-model
consumer passed `1792103912938134000-54284`, with only the three standard axioms.
