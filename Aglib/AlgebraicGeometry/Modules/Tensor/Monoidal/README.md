# Symmetric monoidal structure on scheme modules

[LocalIso](LocalIso.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.Modules.Tensor.Monoidal.LocalIso`, defines `AlgebraicGeometry.Scheme.Modules.presheafLocalIso X`, the class of maps of module presheaves over the structure sheaf that become isomorphisms after module sheafification, and proves that it is monoidal (`presheafLocalIso_isMonoidal`). It also provides the instance `isLocalization_associatedSheaf`: module sheafification is a localization at this class. The leaves below consume these instances; none of them adds a hypothesis on the scheme or on the module sheaves.

The coherence source is general category theory and lives outside this directory: [Localization/Monoidal/Induced](../../../../CategoryTheory/Localization/Monoidal/Induced.lean), imported as `Aglib.CategoryTheory.Localization.Monoidal.Induced`, computes the localized tensor maps, associator, unitors and braiding of Mathlib's `LocalizedMonoidal` through the transported comparison `μOf`, in the shape of the equations of `Monoidal.InducingFunctorData` and of `BraidedCategory.ofFaithful` (see the [localization README](../../../../CategoryTheory/Localization/README.md)).

[Associator](Associator.lean) defines the general associator `tensorSheafAssociator X M N O : tensorSheaf X (tensorSheaf X M N) O ≅ tensorSheaf X M (tensorSheaf X N O)` for arbitrary module sheaves: the sheafified presheaf associator conjugated by the sheafified whiskered sheafification units, which are isomorphisms because `presheafLocalIso X` is monoidal. Its descriptions are the defining square `map_unit_whiskerRight_comp_tensorSheafAssociator_hom`, the presheaf-level unit square `associatedSheafUnit_comp_tensorSheafAssociator_hom`, and the pure-tensor formulas `tensorSheafAssociator_hom_tmul` and `tensorSheafAssociator_inv_tmul` in the style of `tensorSheafMap_tmul`.

[Basic](Basic.lean) puts the global instance `monoidalCategory : MonoidalCategory X.Modules` on scheme modules. Its data are the existing maps: `M ⊗ N` is `tensorSheaf X M N`, `⊗ₘ`, `◁` and `▷` are `tensorSheafMap`, the unit is `SheafOfModules.unit X.ringCatSheaf`, `λ_` is `tensorSheafLeftUnit`, `ρ_` is `tensorRightUnitIso` and `α_` is `tensorSheafAssociator`. Each identification is `rfl` (`tensorObj_eq_tensorSheaf`, `tensorHom_eq_tensorSheafMap`, `whiskerLeft_eq_tensorSheafMap`, `whiskerRight_eq_tensorSheafMap`, `tensorUnit_eq_unit`, `leftUnitor_eq_tensorSheafLeftUnit`, `rightUnitor_eq_tensorRightUnitIso`, `associator_eq_tensorSheafAssociator`), as are `moduleTensorLeftFunctor X M = tensorLeft M`, `moduleTensorRightFunctor X M = tensorRight M` and `(moduleTensorCoefficientMap X f).app P = f ▷ P`. The coherence equations come from `Monoidal.induced` along the identity functor `toLocalizedMonoidal X` into Mathlib's `LocalizedMonoidal`, which serves only as the coherence source; that functor is monoidal (`instMonoidalToLocalizedMonoidal`). `associator_hom_tmul` and `associator_inv_tmul` restate the pure-tensor formulas for `α_`. The `rfl` field lemmas are deliberately not `@[simp]`: oriented forward they would rewrite the monoidal spelling back to the legacy names and block Mathlib's monoidal simp set and coherence, and reversed they would rewrite along object identities such as `M ⊗ N = tensorSheaf X M N` that hold only up to unfolding instances; use them with `rw`, `dsimp only` or `change`.

[Symmetric](Symmetric.lean) adds the global instances `braidedCategory` and `symmetricCategory`, with braiding `tensorSheafBraiding` (`braiding_eq_tensorSheafBraiding`, `rfl`), through `BraidedCategory.ofFaithful` and `SymmetricCategory.ofFaithful`; `toLocalizedMonoidal X` is a braided functor.

[Sections](Sections.lean) imports `Symmetric`. It states the associator on nested `moduleTensorSection` generators (`associator_hom_moduleTensorSection`, `associator_inv_moduleTensorSection`), the whiskerings on pure section tensors (`whiskerRight_moduleTensorSection`, `whiskerLeft_moduleTensorSection`), the inverse unitors `m ↦ 1 ⊗ m` and `m ↦ m ⊗ 1` (`leftUnitor_inv_moduleTensorSection`, `rightUnitor_inv_moduleTensorSection`, from the unit formulas `tensorLeftUnitIso_hom_section` and `tensorRightUnitIso_hom_section` of [Tensor/Sections](../Sections.lean)) and the exchange of factors by the braiding (`braiding_hom_moduleTensorSection`), and proves that morphisms out of a nested tensor product of arbitrary module sheaves are determined by their values on nested pure section tensors, in both bracketings (`moduleTensor_nested_hom_ext`, `moduleTensor_nested_hom_ext_left`). These generalize `moduleTensor_nested_hom_ext_of_line`, which needs a line-bundle factor.

Several declarations set `backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false` locally, with a comment, where a proof crosses the `X.ringCatSheaf` and commutative-ring spellings of the structure sheaf. The arguments of `LocalizedMonoidal` are typed on the commutative-ring spelling (`associatedSheafTensorUnitIso`, `toLocalizedMonoidalCounitIso`), so that its instances are found at instance transparency. Consumers need no option to find the monoidal, braided and symmetric instances.

Focused consumers with axiom guards are [AglibTest LocalIso](../../../../../AglibTest/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Tensor/Monoidal/LocalIso.lean), [AglibTest Basic](../../../../../AglibTest/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Tensor/Monoidal/Basic.lean) and [AglibTest Sections](../../../../../AglibTest/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Tensor/Monoidal/Sections.lean).

The monoidal twisting functor `Discrete ℤ ⥤ P.Modules` on projective space is built in [ProjectiveSpace/Tensor/Monoidal](../../../ProjectiveSpace/Tensor/Monoidal/README.md), with tensorator `μIso = twistingTensorIso` and unit comparison `εIso = (twistingZeroIso R ι).symm`; it is braided for the additive braiding of `Discrete ℤ`.
