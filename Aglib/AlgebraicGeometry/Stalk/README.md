# Actual scheme stalks and local comparisons

[Dimension](Dimension.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.Stalk.Dimension`, gives `Scheme.topologicalKrullDim_eq_iSup_stalk` for every scheme: its dimension is the supremum of its actual stalk dimensions. No field, Noetherianity or quasi-compactness is required.

[MapProperties](MapProperties.lean) gives `Scheme.stalkMap_maximalIdeal_liesOver` for every scheme map, using that map's induced algebra. `isNoetherianRing_stalk_of_locallyOfFiniteType` requires local finite type and Noetherianity of the target stalk, without a global Noetherian or etale hypothesis.

[FlatBaseChange](FlatBaseChange.lean) owns `Scheme.Hom.isIso_stalkMap_of_flat_of_surjectiveOnStalks`, `Scheme.fromSpecStalk_flat` and `Scheme.Hom.stalkMapIsoOfIsPullbackOfFlatOfPreimmersion`. The arrow isomorphism uses the supplied pullback square, flat preimmersion base map and actual point/equality. It compares the original stalk maps; no etale hypothesis is imposed.

[Polynomial](Polynomial.lean) gives actual polynomial-scheme stalk regularity, the all-prime dimension upper bound and the maximal-prime equality over an arbitrary field. [EtalePolynomial](EtalePolynomial.lean) supplies the corresponding three results for an actual etale map from that polynomial algebra. Dimension equality retains the maximal-ideal premise; no perfectness or separability is required. Its maximal-comap ring lemma belongs in [RingTheory/Etale](../../RingTheory/Etale/README.md).

[GenericFiber](GenericFiber.lean) retains `Scheme.LocalRing.GenericFiber` for the actual pullback of `p : Y ⟶ Spec A` to a supplied field `F` with `IsFractionRing A F`. Its projection's own stalk map gives `projection_stalkMap_isIso` and `stalkEquiv`. The general flat-stalk supplier is the lower `FlatBaseChange` interface; no global integrality condition on `Y` is added.

The lower stalk interfaces feed [etale transport and neighborhoods](../Morphisms/Etale/README.md) and [smooth regularity/dimension](../Morphisms/Smooth/README.md). Their focused imports separate arbitrary-scheme stalk theory from these stronger morphism specializations.

[IntegralExtension](IntegralExtension.lean) equates the dimensions of actual Spec stalks under an integral ring extension with going down, at the actual contracted prime. Its finite-product form retains the supplied algebra equivalence and both hypotheses on every factor. No domain, Noetherianity, dimension bound or finite-map hypothesis is added. [Generic finite-etale normalization dimensions](../Normalization/GenericFiniteEtale/Dimension.lean) apply this result to the actual integral-closure field factors.

[Scalar](Scalar.lean) owns `Scheme.Hom.stalkScalar`, the literal global-constant/pullback/germ
map over any commutative-ring base. `stalkScalar_eq_local` compares it with the
actual local-base germ and stalk map. Flatness of the structural morphism gives
`stalkScalar_flat` and `stalkScalar_mem_nonZeroDivisors` without a field, domain,
Noetherian, DVR or dimension hypothesis. Field stalk constants transparently delegate to this common map.

[SpecLocalRing](SpecLocalRing.lean) owns
`Scheme.fromSpecStalk_comp_eq_spec_map_stalkScalar`,
`Scheme.spec_map_comp_fromSpecStalk_injective` and
`Scheme.exists_spec_map_comp_fromSpecStalk`. They compare the actual structural
scalar map and canonical stalk morphism and give the fixed-point existence and
uniqueness forms of Mathlib's `SpecToEquivOfLocalRing`. The commutative-ring,
local-ring and local-map hypotheses remain explicit. The general infinitesimal
smoothness criterion and Picard tangent points use this shared construction.

[PrincipalBaseChange](PrincipalBaseChange.lean) proves
`Scheme.Hom.ker_stalkMap_pullback_fst_of_principal`. A surjective commutative
base-ring map with kernel generated by `a` has exactly that principal kernel
on every original projection stalk of the literal scheme pullback. The
generator is the image under `Scheme.Hom.stalkScalar`; projection injectivity
identifies the constructed point with the supplied point. No flatness, domain
or Noetherian condition is required.

[DedekindBase](DedekindBase.lean), in `Scheme.DedekindBase`, constructs actual localization-to-stalk,
vertical height-one and horizontal fraction-field maps for schemes over a
Dedekind domain. The field/scalar compatibility comes from the actual generic
stalk map and the canonical equivalence with the supplied fraction-field
model. The source/target integrality and dominance assumptions stay explicit
where used. [GenericSection](GenericSection.lean), in `Scheme.GenericSection`, constructs the actual
base change of a supplied section, its rational generic origin and original
image/stalk/residue comparisons; the integral and Dedekind specializations
retain their exact source conditions. The common formula
`Scheme.LocalRing.GenericFiber.baseMap_closedPoint_eq_genericPoint` is in this
leaf and requires a domain and its actual supplied fraction field. These
constructions have no arithmetic-model existence premise or conclusion.

The [FlatDVR guide](FlatDVR/README.md) records
`Scheme.FlatDVR.flatDVRFibreComponentKernel`, the supplier of the original
`FlatDVRFibreComponentKernel` family. For an integral closed component of the
literal special fibre of a flat DVR scheme, the original stalk kernel is
minimal above the actual uniformizer ideal at every component point. The two
generic kernel lemmas above supply minimality; flatness preserves the
uniformizer as a nonzerodivisor. The retained family keeps its original locally
Noetherian hypothesis and supplies the existing dimension-one/DVR consumers.
Broader surface dimension and regularity interfaces remain separate.
[PrincipalColength](PrincipalColength.lean) and the
[codimension-one provider](CodimensionOne.lean) preserve the actual stalk and
residue maps used by curve norm and degree constructions.

[LocalBaseIdeal](LocalBaseIdeal.lean) compares the actual section-scalar map
followed by its germ with the original `stalkScalar`. For a scheme over a
local ring and a point mapping to the closed point of the base, that structural
stalk map is local. Consequently every proper base ideal has proper image in
the actual stalk; the same holds for the induced affine section ideal after
the original germ. No properness of the scheme map, Noetherianity, completeness,
or flatness is required. The
[finite-etale good-open construction](../Morphisms/FiniteEtale/Adic/CoherentGoodOpens.lean)
uses this proper-ideal condition in the finite local etaleness criterion.
