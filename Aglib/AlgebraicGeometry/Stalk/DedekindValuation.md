# DedekindValuation

`Scheme.valuationRing_stalk_of_isDedekindDomain` applies at every actual point
of the spectrum of a Dedekind domain. Its generic stalk is handled by the
field case. The nonfield case is a DVR by the localization theorem. The
conclusion is `ValuationRing`, without a claim that every stalk is a DVR.
