# The twisting sheaves as a braided monoidal functor

This family puts the twisting sheaves `O(n) = twistingSheaf R ι n` of polynomial projective space, over every commutative ring `R` and coordinate type `ι`, into the symmetric monoidal structure of scheme modules. No hypothesis on `R` or `ι` is added. Its imports are:

- the scheme-module layer [Modules/Tensor/Monoidal](../../../Modules/Tensor/Monoidal/README.md) through its `Sections` leaf, which imports `Symmetric` and supplies the associator, whiskerings, inverse unitors and braiding on pure section tensors, and [Modules/Tensor/Sections](../../../Modules/Tensor/Sections.lean) for `moduleTensorSection` and `moduleTensorCoefficientMap_section`;
- the general category theory of [CategoryTheory/Monoidal](../../../../CategoryTheory/Monoidal/README.md): the additive braiding `Discrete.addBraidedCategory` of `Discrete ℤ` (`Discrete`) and multiplication by a morphism out of the unit, `unitMultiply`, with its coherence lemmas (`UnitMultiplication`);
- Mathlib's `Mathlib.CategoryTheory.Monoidal.Discrete`;
- the projective-space leaves `Tensor.Isomorphism` (twist multiplication `twistingTensorMap`/`twistingTensorIso`), `Tensor.Unit` (`twistingZeroIso`, `unitToTwistingZero`), `Multiplication` (`twistingSectionMul`, `twistingSectionsEquiv_mul`, `twistingMultiplyGlobal`), `Hom.Sections` (`unitHomGlobalSectionsEquiv`, `twistingGlobalSectionsHomAddEquiv`) and `Tensor.Equivalence.Sections` (`twistingTensorCompIso` and its section formula). Through them it uses the frame equivalences `twistingSectionsEquiv` and the transport lemmas `twistingSection_eqToHom_val`, `twistingSectionsEquiv_eqToHom` of `Twisting`, and `hom_ext_of_coordinateSubopens` of `Twisting.Frames`. `Tensor.Equivalence.Sections` imports the invertible-sheaf leaf `Modules.Invertible.TensorFunctorAssociator.Sections`, so `TensorFunctors` depends on the Invertible layer; the other leaves do not import it directly.

[Functor](Functor.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.ProjectiveSpace.Tensor.Monoidal.Functor`, defines `twistingFunctor R ι : Discrete ℤ ⥤ (ProjectiveSpace R ι).Modules`, `n ↦ O(n.as)`, whose maps are the transports `eqToHom (congrArg (twistingSheaf R ι) _)`. The instance `twistingFunctor_monoidal` comes from `twistingFunctorCoreMonoidal`, with `μIso m n = twistingTensorIso R ι m.as n.as` and `εIso = (twistingZeroIso R ι).symm` (`twistingFunctor_μ`, `_δ`, `_ε`, `_η`, `_μIso`, `_εIso`, all `rfl`). The coherence equations are proved once at variable degrees: `twistingTensorMap_associativity` by nested pure tensors (`moduleTensor_nested_hom_ext_left`), and `twistingTensorMap_leftUnitor`, `twistingTensorMap_rightUnitor` by frame coefficients on coordinate subopens (`hom_ext_of_coordinateSubopens`). The degree equality enters as `eqToHom` with a free proof, and transport is evaluated only by the `subst` lemmas `twistingSection_eqToHom_val` and `twistingSectionsEquiv_eqToHom` of `Twisting`.

[Braided](Braided.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.ProjectiveSpace.Tensor.Monoidal.Braided`, proves that twist multiplication is compatible with the braidings (`twistingTensorMap_braiding`), using the braiding on pure section tensors (`braiding_hom_moduleTensorSection`) and commutativity of twist multiplication after transport (`twistingSectionMul_comm`), and adds the instance `twistingFunctor_braided : (twistingFunctor R ι).Braided` for `Discrete.addBraidedCategory`.

[Multiplication](Multiplication.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.ProjectiveSpace.Tensor.Monoidal.Multiplication`, identifies a global section `s` of `O(a)` with `twistingSectionUnitHom s : 𝟙_ ⟶ O(a)` through the existing `unitHomGlobalSectionsEquiv` (`twistingGlobalSectionsUnitHomEquiv`), and proves the bridge `twistingMultiplyGlobal_eq_unitMultiply`: `twistingMultiplyGlobal a b s = unitMultiply (twistingSectionUnitHom s) O(b) ≫ twistingTensorMap a b`, that is `(λ_ _).inv ≫ twistingSectionUnitHom s ▷ O(b) ≫ μ_{a,b}` (`twistingMultiplyGlobal_eq`); hence `twistingGlobalSectionsHomAddEquiv` is whiskering followed by `μ`. The leaf evaluates sections only in this bridge and in its input `twistingSectionUnitHom_app_one`, which says that the unit morphism of `s` sends `1` to the restriction of `s`. The multiplication laws on an outer factor (`twistingMultiplyGlobal_whiskerRight_tensorMap`, transport along `a + (d + b) = a + d + b`) and on an inner factor (`whiskerLeft_twistingMultiplyGlobal_tensorMap`, transport along `a + (e + b) = e + (a + b)`) are the coherence lemmas of `UnitMultiplication` for the braided twisting functor.

[Multiply](Multiply.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.ProjectiveSpace.Tensor.Monoidal.Multiply`, defines `twistingTensorMultiply a d s = moduleTensorCoefficientMap _ (twistingMultiplyGlobal a d s) : T_d ⟶ T_{a+d}`, multiplication by a global section `s` of `O(a)` on the twist factor of `T_d = moduleTensorLeftFunctor _ O(d)`, and its value on pure tensors (`twistingTensorMultiply_section`). It imports only `ProjectiveSpace.Multiplication` and `Modules.Tensor.Sections`; the Hilbert-polynomial leaves import it.

[TensorFunctors](TensorFunctors.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.ProjectiveSpace.Tensor.Monoidal.TensorFunctors`, compares the twist tensor functors with the monoidal structure: the component of `twistingTensorCompIso a b` at `F` is `(α_ O(a) O(b) F).inv ≫ twistingTensorMap a b ▷ F` (`twistingTensorCompIso_hom_app`, checked on nested pure tensors), and the transport of twist tensor functors along an equality of degrees is the whiskered transport of twists (`twistingTensorFunctorCongr_hom_app`). Multiplication by a global section on the twist factor is compatible with `twistingTensorCompIso` on the outer factor (`twistingMultiplyGlobal_coefficientMap_app_tensor_comp`) and on the inner factor (`twistingMultiplyGlobal_coefficientMap_map_tensor_comp`). Both proofs are associator naturality and the two multiplication laws; no section is evaluated and no transport is unfolded. The same comparisons stated with `twistingTensorMultiply` are `twistingTensorMultiply_app_tensor_comp` and `twistingTensorMultiply_map_tensor_comp`.

Several declarations set `backward.isDefEq.respectTransparency false` locally, with a comment, where a proof crosses the `X.ringCatSheaf` and module-sheaf spellings of the structure sheaf or the spellings `(moduleTensorLeftFunctor X M).obj N` and `M ⊗ N` of a tensor product, as in the twisting leaves. There is no file-wide option and no heartbeat change. State twist identities at variable degrees and instantiate the degrees last, as the concrete-degree example of the TensorFunctors test does, rather than elaborating a composite at concrete degrees from scratch.

Focused consumers with axiom guards are [AglibTest Functor](../../../../../AglibTest/AlgebraicGeometry/ProjectiveSpace/Tensor/Monoidal/Functor.lean), [AglibTest Multiplication](../../../../../AglibTest/AlgebraicGeometry/ProjectiveSpace/Tensor/Monoidal/Multiplication.lean) and [AglibTest TensorFunctors](../../../../../AglibTest/AlgebraicGeometry/ProjectiveSpace/Tensor/Monoidal/TensorFunctors.lean). The last states the outer and inner comparisons in the form attempted in the Hilbert-polynomial regularity work and the whiskering form of `twistingTensorMultiply` and `twistingTensorMultiplyTo`. The pure-tensor lemmas of scheme modules are guarded in [AglibTest Modules Sections](../../../../../AglibTest/AlgebraicGeometry/Modules/Tensor/Monoidal/Sections.lean).
