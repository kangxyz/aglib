# Actual Cartier powers and their original scalar quotients

The tensor powers, closed ideal powers, scalar multiples and quotients are compared
through their original inclusions. The resulting short exact layers use the actual
exceptional curve and its native conormal O(1).

This focused module is part of the original exceptional-curve contraction argument. It
retains the hypotheses and canonical maps of the supplied formal-neighborhood source; it
does not independently assert the full contraction theorem.
