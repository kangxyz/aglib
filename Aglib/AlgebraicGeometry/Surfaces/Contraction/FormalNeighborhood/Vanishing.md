# Vanishing for the original line-coefficient quotients

The actual coefficient quotients have successive O_E(n+a) layers. For a ≥ −1, their H1
vanishes, and actual proper formal functions plus finite Nakayama gives vanishing of the
original dual Cartier ideal. Compactness and separatedness are derived from the proper
map in that endpoint.

This focused module is part of the original exceptional-curve contraction argument. It
retains the hypotheses and canonical maps of the supplied formal-neighborhood source; it
does not independently assert the full contraction theorem.
