# The original blowup of a rational surface point is normal

Assume the original scheme has normal stalks and x is closed. If its actual
stalk is Nagata, has dimension two, and satisfies genuine normal-modification
H1 vanishing, the whole original point blowup has normal stalks.

Nagata supplies Noetherianity only at that actual stalk, and original normality
supplies its domain and integrally closed instances. No global integrality,
Noetherianity, properness, or regularity is assumed. The proof uses the existing
ordinary local rational-blowup normality theorem and the existing original
flat localization square, then applies the native local-replacement normality
law with the actual unchanged complement. It covers every point of the fibre,
not merely the globally closed singular points.
