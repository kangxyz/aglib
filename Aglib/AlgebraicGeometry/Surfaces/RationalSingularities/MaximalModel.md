# Rationality at actual stalks of a maximal model

`normalModificationH1Vanishing_stalk_of_original_R1_vanishing` applies the
constructed local replacement to every supplied normal proper birational
model of a closed nongeneric stalk. The original Cartesian square and the
original structure-module R1/stalk comparison give its H1 vanishing.

`exists_model_closed_nongeneric_stalk_vanishing` combines the published
maximum-from-bound and original R1 vanishing theorem with this actual local
replacement. Its premise is a genuine uniform H1 bound for a complete
normal Noetherian local domain of dimension two. This module does not
construct that bound, a resolution, or a projective maximizing model.
