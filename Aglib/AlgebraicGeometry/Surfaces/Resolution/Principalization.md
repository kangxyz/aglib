# Finite point-blowup principalization

Import `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.Surfaces.Resolution.Principalization`.

`exists_regular_pointBlowup_principalization` starts with an actual locally Noetherian integral scheme X, regularity of every original stalk, actual stalk dimensions at most two, and a nonzero native ideal sheaf J whose actual closed quotient subscheme is Artinian. It constructs a finite `PointBlowupChain` of the original point blowups. The actual composite is proper, dominant and birational over X, its source is integral and regular with the retained dimension bound, and the original inverse-image ideal has a locally rank-one actual ideal module.

`PointBlowupChain` is an inductive object built from identity maps and the actual constructed blowup maps at closed points with two-dimensional local rings. Its `length` counts the steps. Properness, regularity, dimension, integrality and dominance are proved along this actual chain. `PointBlowupChain.birationalOver` composes the original dense partial isomorphisms over the actual composite map. Source integrality and dominance use the proved `Blowup.Integral` supplier at the original closed nongeneric points; they are not assumed as stage data.

`exists_regular_pointBlowup_principalization_of_dimension_le_two` accepts the standard `topologicalKrullDim X ≤ 2` interface and derives the needed bounds on the original stalks.

The weak ideal and exact Cartier-power factorization come from `Resolution.WeakTransform`. Its chart ideal is the literal saturated-chart weak transform used by the accepted local colength proof. The actual local-spectrum comparison transports strict colength decrease to every original global point over the centre. Away from the centre, the original stalk map is an isomorphism and the original colength and singleton-basis condition are unchanged. Finite total support and finite original local colengths give an actual Artinian weak quotient. The generic-point comparison proves that this weak ideal is nonzero.

`nonfreeIdealStalks` is the finite set of actual ideal stalks without a singleton basis. Nonzero ideals in regular local rings of dimension at most one already have such a basis, so every selected centre has dimension two. `nonfreeIdealColengths` records the natural lengths at these actual points, with multiplicity. `nonfreeIdealColengths_pointWeakTransform_lt` proves an actual Dershowitz-Manna decrease: the selected centre entry is replaced by the finitely many strictly smaller new entries, while all entries away from the centre correspond under the original map. No decreasing sum of unweighted lengths is asserted.

Well-founded induction constructs the terminal chain. An empty nonfree-stalk set gives local rank one of the original embedded ideal module. At each inductive step, `cartierPullback_of_pointWeakTransform` uses the existing canonical Cartier avoidance map and the original source isomorphism to preserve the actual exceptional ideal under the later dominant integral point-chain map. The exact product and power equations recover the original total inverse-image ideal; abstract pullback of an invertible module alone is not used.

The generic actual-stalk/finite-length-to-Artinian quotient bridges now belong to `IdealSheaf.Artinian`, under its native `Scheme.IdealSheafData` namespace. This source does not principalize an arbitrary ideal with divisorial support, construct rational-coordinate base ideals, or assert common regular domination. Those consumers require their original divisorial/base-ideal and generic-map bridges.
