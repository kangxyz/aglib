# Finite domination and original projective coordinates

[FiniteDomination.lean](FiniteDomination.lean) constructs the actual row ideal,
its original kernel-lift sections, and their section frames from the
coefficient-ideal construction. It uses the polynomial
coefficient-product results and the original normalized-cover interfaces.

The row ideal is the literal inverse image of the ideal generated by the
original finite row over B. Its coordinate maps are kernel lifts of the
original global multiplication maps. Affine ideal generation proves that
their coproduct map is epimorphic.

Given the original polynomial relation F=p_row Q and local rank one of the
actual inverse image of content(F), a nonzero local frame t gives
F=C(t)G with content(G)=1. The same-field normal-local polynomial theorem
then gives a nonzero generator of the actual row ideal at every stalk.
Singleton bases of the original closed-immersion stalk kernels prove local
rank one of the actual row module. FromSections constructs its original
section frames and projective map, preserving the original B-structure.

The actual pulled row-ideal inclusion is nonzero over the original field
point, hence an isomorphism of line modules there. FromSections naturality
and its original field-ratio theorem prove equality with the supplied
generic chart as a scheme morphism. The existing closed-immersion
factorization theorem then supplies the map through the original Chow
source, retaining both generic and structural equations.

The endpoint exists_finite_domination starts with the original finite
injective A-algebra B, the spectral map s identified with that same algebra
map, its actual arbitrary finite function-field extension, and any actual
integral proper birational g:T→Spec B. It constructs the Chow source,
extracts its original field coordinates, clears their denominators, and
contracts the resulting nonzero polynomial to A[Z]. The coefficient ideal
of this polynomial is the actual center passed to NormalizedBlowupCover.
The row construction above is then applied to that same constructed Y.

The result consists of normal integral X,Y and original maps a,b,π,h with
a,b,h proper and birational on their original function-field maps, π finite
and dominant by its actual relative-normalization construction,
h≫g=b, and π≫a=b≫s. The native statement requires A Noetherian Nagata of
dimension at most two and B Noetherian; locality and normality of the
original base rings are not needed in this geometric producer. The finite
extension and the original structural square remain explicit. Separability
is absent from the endpoint's hypotheses. Finiteness, normality, local
Noetherianity, and properness of the same existing cover use the
`NormalizedBlowupCover.Nagata` wrappers and their original field action.
No row-principality, projective extension, resolution, finite diagram,
or flattening theorem is an input. No flatness of π is claimed.

The construction retains `FiniteBase.Y`, `pi`, `b`, and `eta`, the Chow
factor h, and both original squares. It supplies geometric finite domination;
the cohomological finite-extension bound has its own consumer.
