# Étale cohomological dimension

[SharpProperDimension](SharpProperDimension.lean) proves that for a proper
morphism with ordinary fibres of dimension at most `d`, the higher direct
image of an internally torsion abelian sheaf vanishes in degree `q > 2 * d`.
The base is arbitrary. [SharpFiniteDimension](SharpFiniteDimension.lean)
proves the corresponding absolute bound over a algebraically closed field.
