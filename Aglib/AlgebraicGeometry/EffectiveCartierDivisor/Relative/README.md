# Relative effective Cartier divisors

[Basic](Basic.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.EffectiveCartierDivisor.Relative.Basic`, defines `Scheme.Hom.IsEffectiveCartier i`, a closed immersion whose actual ideal module `idealModule i` is locally rank one (Kleiman, *The Picard scheme*, arXiv:math/0504020v1, 3.1; Stacks Project, Tag 01WR), and `Scheme.Hom.IsRelativeEffectiveCartier i p`, which adds `Flat (i ≫ p)` (Kleiman, Def. 3.3; Stacks Project, Tag 062T). `Scheme.Hom.FiberIsEffectiveCartier i p t` is the condition on the actual fiber inclusion `fibreInclusion p i (T.fromSpecResidueField t)`. Relative effective Cartier divisors are stable under every cartesian base change of the base (`IsRelativeEffectiveCartier.of_isPullback`; Stacks Project, Tag 056Q) and transport along isomorphisms (`IsRelativeEffectiveCartier.of_iso`); in particular their fibers are effective Cartier. No hypothesis on `p` is needed there.

Noetherianity of `A` spreads regularity from a stalk to a principal open. |
| `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.EffectiveCartierDivisor.Relative.Open` | `isOpen_setOf_fiberIsEffectiveCartier` for a locally Noetherian base, `p` flat, locally of finite type and universally closed, `i` a closed immersion and `D` flat over the base, with the open `fiberCartierLocus i p`. `isRelativeEffectiveCartier_restrict(_fiberCartierLocus)`: over every open inside the good set `D` is relative effective Cartier (no closedness needed). The locally Noetherian base supplies the finite-presentation conditions and the spreading of regularity. This is the first paragraph of the proof of Kleiman, Thm 3.7. |
| `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.EffectiveCartierDivisor.Relative.BaseChange` | For any cartesian squares `X' = X ×_T T'`, `D' = D ×_X X'` along `g : T' ⟶ T`: `fiberIsEffectiveCartier_iff_of_isPullback` (`i` closed, `D` flat; by faithfully flat descent along the map of fibers) and `isRelativeEffectiveCartier_iff_of_isPullback` (`i` a closed immersion, `p` and `D ⟶ T` flat and locally of finite presentation, any `T'`). `isRelativeEffectiveCartier_iff_range_subset_of_isPullback` and `..._iff_exists_lift_of_isPullback`: for a locally Noetherian base, `p` flat, locally of finite type and universally closed, `i` a closed immersion and `D` flat over the base, `D'` is relative effective Cartier iff `g` lands in, equivalently factors through, `fiberCartierLocus i p`. The `fibreInclusion` forms state the canonical base change. `isLocallyRankOne_idealModule_of_pullback` descends local rank one of a closed ideal along affine flat surjective morphisms. This is the representability paragraph of the proof of Kleiman, Thm 3.7, for an arbitrary family. |
| `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.EffectiveCartierDivisor.Relative.ZeroScheme` | Integral geometric fibres plus a nonzero fibre map `φ : L ⟶ O` give `GeometricFibreRegular` (`geometricFibreRegular_of_isIntegral_of_ne_zero`). For `f` flat, locally of finite presentation and `GeometricallyIntegral`, the zero scheme is a relative effective Cartier divisor (`isRelativeEffectiveCartier_zeroInclusion_of_geometricallyIntegral`, and the section form through `sectionContraction`) (Kleiman, Thm 3.13, p. 26). |

Not included: the instantiation with the Hilbert scheme (`Div` open in `Hilb`), Kleiman's Lemma 3.4 with hypotheses only at one point (including flatness of `X` derived from (i)), the pointwise criterion over non-Noetherian bases (Stacks Project, Tag 062Y (1)–(3)), and the weakening of universal closedness of `p` to closedness of `i ≫ p`.
