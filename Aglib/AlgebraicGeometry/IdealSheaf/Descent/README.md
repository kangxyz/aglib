# Faithfully flat descent of closed subschemes

Pullback of ideal sheaves is Mathlib's `Scheme.IdealSheafData.comap`, the ideal of the schematic inverse image `pullback.fst f I.subschemeι`; pushforward `Scheme.IdealSheafData.map` is the kernel of `I.subschemeι ≫ f`, the ideal of the scheme-theoretic image. No replacement ideal-sheaf or descent-datum type is introduced.

[Pullback](Pullback.lean), imported as `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.IdealSheaf.Descent.Pullback`, collects the properties of these operations that descent uses. A flat surjective morphism has injective section maps (`Scheme.Hom.app_injective_of_flat_of_surjective`) and trivial kernel (instance `IsSchemeTheoreticallyDominant.of_flat_of_surjective`); neither needs quasi-compactness, because the stalk maps are flat local, hence faithfully flat. Hence `(I.comap f).map f = I` along a flat surjective `f` (`Scheme.IdealSheafData.map_comap_of_flat_of_surjective`), and `comap · f` is injective. Between affine schemes, pullback and pushforward are extension and contraction of ideals of global sections along `f.appTop` (`ideal_comap_top_of_isAffine`, `ideal_map_top_of_isAffine`). The kernel of a quasi-compact morphism commutes with base change along an open immersion (`Scheme.Hom.ker_eq_comap_of_isPullback_of_isOpenImmersion`), and ideal sheaves agreeing after pullback along a jointly surjective family of open immersions are equal (`Scheme.IdealSheafData.ext_of_comap_eq`).

[FaithfullyFlat](FaithfullyFlat.lean) proves Nitsure's Lemma 6.7(2) (arXiv:math/0504590v1, p. 32, which Nitsure attributes to SGA 1, Exposé VIII, Corollaire 1.9, not checked here; compare Stacks Project Tag 03I0, effective descent of closed immersions along fpqc coverings). Let `f : X ⟶ Y` be flat, surjective and quasi-compact and let `Z` be an ideal sheaf on `X` with `Z.comap (pullback.fst f f) = Z.comap (pullback.snd f f)`. Then `Scheme.IdealSheafData.comap_map_eq_self_of_flat_of_surjective` gives `(Z.map f).comap f = Z`, and `existsUnique_comap_eq_of_flat_of_surjective` gives uniqueness of the descended ideal sheaf on `Y`; `exists_comap_eq_iff_of_flat_of_surjective` adds the converse. In closed-immersion form, `isPullback_lift_ker_comp_of_flat_of_surjective` shows that a closed immersion `i : T ⟶ X` with equal pullbacks is the base change along `f` of the scheme-theoretic image `(i ≫ f).ker` of `T` in `Y`, and `flat_surjective_quasiCompact_lift_ker_comp_of_flat_of_surjective` that the induced morphism `T ⟶ (i ≫ f).ker.subscheme` is flat, surjective and quasi-compact. For uniqueness, `ker_eq_ker_comp_of_isPullback_of_flat_of_surjective` identifies any closed subscheme of `Y` whose base change along a flat surjective `f` is `i` with that scheme-theoretic image. `comap_comap_fst_eq_comap_comap_snd_of_comp_eq` transfers the descent condition along commutative squares.

The proof first passes to an affine target through Mathlib's affine open cover of `Y`, using the open base change of kernels and joint injectivity along open covers. Over an affine target it passes to the finite disjoint union of an affine cover of `X`, which is affine and flat surjective over `Y`. Between affine schemes it applies the ring statement `Ideal.map_comap_eq_self_of_isPushout` of [RingTheory/Descent](../../../RingTheory/Descent/README.md) to Mathlib's pushout square of global sections, `isPushout_appTop_of_isPullback`.

The coequalizer half, Lemma 6.7(1), is supplied by Mathlib: flat surjective quasi-compact morphisms are `EffectiveEpi` (`Mathlib/AlgebraicGeometry/Sites/Fpqc.lean`), and `CategoryTheory.isColimitCoforkOfEffectiveEpi` makes `f` the coequalizer of the two projections `X ×_Y X ⟶ X`. The focused test `AglibTest.AlgebraicGeometry.IdealSheaf.Descent.FaithfullyFlat` records this form. The intended consumer is the quotient by a flat proper equivalence relation (Nitsure, proof of Theorem 6.8, pp. 34–35), applied to the graph of the classifying map inside the universal family.
