# Ideal quotients and actual stalk ideals

Import `Aglib.AlgebraicGeometry.IdealSheaf.Colon`.

`Scheme.IdealSheafData.stalkIdeal` is the ideal of the original local ring generated by germs of sections on affine neighbourhoods. `stalkIdeal_eq_map` identifies it with extension along the original germ from every affine neighbourhood. Inclusion and equality are detected on these actual stalk ideals. Pullback, multiplication and powers retain their actual maps.

For a locally Noetherian scheme, `IdealSheafData.colon I K` constructs a native ideal sheaf whose ideal on each affine open is the ordinary ideal quotient. The localization proof uses finite generation of the denominator ideal: singleton quotients commute with localization by clearing one denominator, and finite generation reduces the general statement to finite intersections. No compatibility with arbitrary infinitely generated denominators is asserted. The construction is on the affine basis; it does not identify a quotient sheaf's sections on arbitrary nonaffine opens with the quotient of their section ideals.

`stalkIdeal_colon` gives the ordinary quotient in every original stalk ring. `comap_colon_of_isIso_stalkMap` gives pullback compatibility when the actual stalk maps are isomorphisms; in particular this holds for open immersions. It does not assert unrestricted pullback compatibility.

The current consumer is the weak ideal on the actual point blowup. This module does not supply its finite support, strict colength descent or global principalization.
