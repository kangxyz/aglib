# Polynomial Milnor residues

`Basic` constructs `MilnorK.PolynomialResidue.residueAt` at the literal
irreducible-polynomial DVR, with its actual root-field identification. `Support`
proves finite support and constructs the direct-sum total residue. Restriction,
coefficient change, linear places, tensor branches and Laurent-series completion
leaves compare the original maps under their displayed hypotheses.

Rational-function exactness and polynomial norms consume this family. Generic
polynomial and local-ring prerequisites remain in their lower algebraic owners.
